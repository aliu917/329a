CORRECT SOLUTION STEPS:
Step 0: Define the variables involved: Let \( PQ = 735 \), \( QR = 112 \), and \( RC \) be the length we want to find.
Step 1: Recognize that triangles \( \triangle PAQ \) and \( \triangle PDC \) are similar, which allows us to set up a proportion of corresponding sides.
Step 2: Set up the proportion from the similarity of triangles: 
\[
\frac{AQ}{CD} = \frac{PQ}{PC} = \frac{735}{112 + 735 + RC} = \frac{735}{847 + RC}.
\]
Step 3: Recognize that triangles \( \triangle BRQ \) and \( \triangle DRC \) are also similar, leading to another proportion:
\[
\frac{QR}{RC} = \frac{112}{RC} = \frac{CD - AQ}{CD} = 1 - \frac{AQ}{CD}.
\]
Step 4: Express \( \frac{AQ}{CD} \) in terms of \( RC \):
\[
\frac{AQ}{CD} = 1 - \frac{112}{RC} = \frac{RC - 112}{RC}.
\]
Step 5: Equate the two expressions for \( \frac{AQ}{CD} \):
\[
\frac{735}{847 + RC} = \frac{RC - 112}{RC}.
\]
Step 6: Cross-multiply to eliminate the fractions:
\[
735RC = (RC + 847)(RC - 112).
\]
Step 7: Expand the right side:
\[
735RC = RC^2 - 112RC + 847RC - 847 \cdot 112.
\]
This simplifies to:
\[
735RC = RC^2 + 735RC - 847 \cdot 112.
\]
Step 8: Rearrange the equation to isolate zero:
\[
0 = RC^2 - 847 \cdot 112.
\]
Step 9: Solve for \( RC \):
\[
RC = \sqrt{847 \cdot 112}.
\]
Step 10: Calculate \( RC \):
\[
RC = \sqrt{847 \cdot 112} = 308.
\]

Final answer: \( \boxed{308} \)
