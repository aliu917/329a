Since \$A = (u,v)\$, we can find the coordinates of the other points: \$B = (v,u)\$, \$C = (-v,u)\$, \$D = (-v,-u)\$, \$E = (v,-u)\$. If we graph those points, we notice that since the latter four points are all reflected across the x/y-axis, they form a rectangle, and \$ABE\$ is a triangle. The area of \$BCDE\$ is \$(2u)(2v) = 4uv\$ and the area of \$ABE\$ is \$\frac{1}{2}(2u)(u-v) = u^2 - uv\$. Adding these together, we get \$u^2 + 3uv = u(u+3v) = 451 = 11 \cdot 41\$. Since \$u,v\$ are positive, \$u+3v>u\$, and by matching factors we get either \$(u,v) = (1,150)\$ or \$(11,10)\$. Since \$v < u\$ the latter case is the answer, and \$u+v = \boxed{021}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Define the coordinates of point A as \( A = (u, v) \) where \( 0 < v < u \).

Step 1: Determine the coordinates of point B as the reflection of A across the line \( y = x \), which gives \( B = (v, u) \).

Step 2: Find the coordinates of point C as the reflection of B across the y-axis, resulting in \( C = (-v, u) \).

Step 3: Find the coordinates of point D as the reflection of C across the x-axis, resulting in \( D = (-v, -u) \).

Step 4: Find the coordinates of point E as the reflection of D across the y-axis, resulting in \( E = (v, -u) \).

Step 5: Recognize that points B, C, D, and E form a rectangle while points A and E form a triangle.

Step 6: Calculate the area of rectangle BCDE: The dimensions are \( 2u \) (height) and \( 2v \) (width), so the area is \( 4uv \).

Step 7: Calculate the area of triangle ABE: The base is \( 2u \) and the height is \( u - v \), giving an area of \( \frac{1}{2}(2u)(u-v) = u^2 - uv \).

Step 8: Combine the areas to find the total area of shape ABCDE: 
\[
\text{Total Area} = 4uv + (u^2 - uv) = u^2 + 3uv.
\]

Step 9: Set the total area equal to 451:
\[
u^2 + 3uv = 451.
\]

Step 10: Factor the equation: 
\[
u(u + 3v) = 451.
\]

Step 11: Factor 451 to find possible pairs of \( (u, v) \): \( 451 = 11 \cdot 41 \).

Step 12: Consider the factors:
1. \( (u, v) = (1, 150) \)
2. \( (u, v) = (11, 10) \)

Step 13: Since \( v < u \), discard \( (1, 150) \) and keep \( (11, 10) \).

Step 14: Calculate \( u + v \):
\[
