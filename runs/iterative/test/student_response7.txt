Let's denote the two numbers by \( x \) and \( y \) where \( x > y \). We are given two equations based on the problem statement:

1. The sum of the two numbers is 6:
   \[
   x + y = 6
   \]

2. The difference of their squares is 12:
   \[
   x^2 - y^2 = 12
   \]

We can use the identity for the difference of squares, which states \( x^2 - y^2 = (x + y)(x - y) \). Substituting the first equation into this identity, we get:
\[
(x + y)(x - y) = 12
\]
\[
6(x - y) = 12
\]

Now, solve for \( x - y \):
\[
x - y = \frac{12}{6} = 2
\]

The positive difference of the two numbers is:
\[
\boxed{2}
\]
