Let's denote the two numbers as \( x \) and \( y \) with \( x > y \).

1. **Sum of the Numbers:**
   \[
   x + y = 6
   \]

2. **Difference of Their Squares:**
   \[
   x^2 - y^2 = 12
   \]

3. **Factoring the Difference of Squares:**
   We can factor \( x^2 - y^2 \) using the difference of squares formula:
   \[
   x^2 - y^2 = (x + y)(x - y)
   \]
   Substituting the sum of the numbers from the first equation:
   \[
   (x + y)(x - y) = 12
   \]
   Since \( x + y = 6 \), we substitute this into the equation:
   \[
   6(x - y) = 12
   \]

4. **Solving for \( x - y \):**
   Divide both sides by 6:
   \[
   x - y = \frac{12}{6} = 2
   \]

5. **Conclusion:**
   The positive difference between the two numbers is:
   \[
   \boxed{2}
   \]

By following these steps, we have clearly maintained a logical flow and related each step back to the original equations and conditions.
