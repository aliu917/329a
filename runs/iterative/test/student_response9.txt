Let's denote the two numbers as \( x \) and \( y \) where \( x > y \).

From the problem statement, we have two key pieces of information:
1. The sum of the two numbers is 6:
   \[
   x + y = 6
   \]

2. The difference of their squares is 12:
   \[
   x^2 - y^2 = 12
   \]

We can use the difference of squares formula, which states:
\[
x^2 - y^2 = (x + y)(x - y)
\]

Substituting the given sum of the numbers \( x + y = 6 \) into the difference of squares formula, we get:
\[
12 = 6(x - y)
\]

Next, solve for \( x - y \):
\[
x - y = \frac{12}{6} = 2
\]

Thus, the positive difference between the two numbers is:
\[
\boxed{2}
\]
