To find the area of the trapezoid, we start by identifying the given information and the formula for the area of a trapezoid. The area \(A\) of a trapezoid is given by:

\[ A = \frac{1}{2} \times (b_1 + b_2) \times h \]

where \(b_1\) and \(b_2\) are the lengths of the two bases, and \(h\) is the height.

From the problem, we know:
- One base \(b_1\) is twice the height \(x\), so \(b_1 = 2x\).
- The other base \(b_2\) is three times the height \(x\), so \(b_2 = 3x\).
- The height \(h\) of the trapezoid is \(x\).

Now, substitute these values into the area formula:

\[ A = \frac{1}{2} \times (2x + 3x) \times x \]

Simplify the expression inside the parentheses:

\[ A = \frac{1}{2} \times 5x \times x \]

\[ A = \frac{1}{2} \times 5x^2 \]

\[ A = \frac{5x^2}{2} \]

Thus, the expression for the area of the trapezoid in terms of the height \(x\) is:

\[\boxed{\frac{5x^2}{2}}\]
