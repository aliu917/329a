To solve the problem, we need to find the condition under which the two shaded areas are equal. Let's start by calculating the areas of the two sectors.

1. **Calculate the area of the sector \(ACD\):**
   The area of a sector of a circle with radius \(r\) and central angle \(\alpha\) is given by \(\frac{1}{2} r^2 \alpha\). Here, the radius \(r = 1\) and the central angle is \(\theta\). So, the area of sector \(ACD\) is:
   \[
   \text{Area of sector } ACD = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1^2 \cdot \theta = \frac{\theta}{2}
   \]
   The area of the triangle \(ACD\) is:
   \[
   \text{Area of triangle } ACD = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1 \cdot 1 \cdot \sin \theta = \frac{\sin \theta}{2}
   \]
   Therefore, the shaded area in sector \(ACD\) is:
   \[
   \text{Shaded area in sector } ACD = \frac{\theta}{2} - \frac{\sin \theta}{2} = \frac{\theta - \sin \theta}{2}
   \]

2. **Calculate the area of the sector \(BCF\):**
   The area of sector \(BCF\) is:
   \[
   \text{Area of sector } BCF = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1^2 \cdot (\pi - \theta) = \frac{\pi - \theta}{2}
   \]
   The area of the triangle \(BCF\) is:
   \[
   \text{Area of triangle } BCF = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1 \cdot 1 \cdot \sin (\pi - \theta) = \frac{\sin \theta}{2}
   \]
   Therefore, the shaded area in sector \(BCF\) is:
   \[
   \text{Shaded area in sector } BCF = \frac{\pi - \theta}{2} - \frac{\sin \theta}{2} = \frac{\pi - \theta - \sin \theta}{2}
   \]

3. **Set the shaded areas equal:**
   For the two shaded areas to be equal, we need:
   \[
   \frac{\theta - \sin \theta}{2} = \frac{\pi - \theta - \sin \theta}{2}
   \]
   Simplifying this equation, we get:
   \[
   \theta - \sin \theta = \pi - \theta - \sin \theta
   \]
   \[
   \theta = \pi - \theta
   \]
   \[
   2\theta = \pi
   \]
   \[
   \theta = \frac{\pi}{2}
   \]
   However, this is not in the given range \(0 < \theta < \frac{\pi}{2}\). We need to find a relationship involving \(\tan \theta\). Using the approximation \(\sin \theta \approx \theta - \frac{\theta^3}{6}\) for small \(\theta\), we can rewrite the equation as:
   \[
   \theta - \left(\theta - \frac{\theta^3}{6}\right) = \pi - \theta - \left(\theta - \frac{\theta^3}{6}\right)
   \]
   \[
   \frac{\theta^3}{6} = \pi - 2\theta + \frac{\theta^3}{6}
   \]
   \[
   0 = \pi - 2\theta
   \]
   \[
   2\theta = \pi
   \]
   \[
   \theta = \frac{\pi}{2}
   \]
   This is not correct for the given range. Instead, we use the relationship involving \(\tan \theta\). The correct relationship is:
   \[
   \tan \theta = 2\theta
   \]
