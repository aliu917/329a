To solve for the minimum value of \( |z| \) where \( z \) is a complex number satisfying \( |z - 3i| + |z - 4| = 5 \), we first interpret the given equation geometrically. The equation \( |z - 3i| + |z - 4| = 5 \) describes the set of points \( z \) in the complex plane such that the sum of the distances from \( z \) to the points \( 3i \) (which is \( (0,3) \)) and \( 4 \) (which is \( (4,0) \)) is constant and equal to 5. This is the definition of an ellipse with foci at \( (0,3) \) and \( (4,0) \) and the major axis length of 5.

The distance between the foci is calculated as follows:
\[
\sqrt{(4-0)^2 + (0-3)^2} = \sqrt{16 + 9} = 5.
\]
Since the distance between the foci is equal to the length of the major axis, the ellipse degenerates into a line segment joining the points \( (0,3) \) and \( (4,0) \).

Next, we need to find the minimum value of \( |z| \), which is the distance from the origin to a point on this line segment. The equation of the line passing through the points \( (0,3) \) and \( (4,0) \) can be found using the two-point form of the equation of a line:
\[
y - 3 = \frac{0-3}{4-0}(x-0) \implies y - 3 = -\frac{3}{4}x \implies y = -\frac{3}{4}x + 3.
\]
We need to minimize \( |z| = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = \sqrt{x^2 + \left(-\frac{3}{4}x + 3\right)^2} \). Substituting \( y = -\frac{3}{4}x + 3 \) into the expression for \( |z| \), we get:
\[
|z| = \sqrt{x^2 + \left(-\frac{3}{4}x + 3\right)^2} = \sqrt{x^2 + \left(\frac{9}{16}x^2 - \frac{9}{2}x + 9\right)} = \sqrt{\frac{25}{16}x^2 - \frac{9}{2}x + 9}.
\]
To find the minimum value, we can use calculus by taking the derivative of the expression inside the square root with respect to \( x \) and setting it to zero. However, we can also use the fact that the minimum distance from the origin to a line is the perpendicular distance from the origin to the line. The perpendicular distance from the origin to the line \( y = -\frac{3}{4}x + 3 \) is given by:
\[
\frac{|0 + \frac{3}{4}(0) - 3|}{\sqrt{\left(-\frac{3}{4}\right)^2 + 1^2}} = \frac{3}{\sqrt{\frac{9}{16} + 1}} = \frac{3}{\sqrt{\frac{25}{16}}} = \frac{3}{\frac{5}{4}} = \frac{12}{5}.
\]
Thus, the minimum value of \( |z| \) is \( \boxed{\frac{12}{5}} \).
