1. **Start with the given equation:**
   \[
   9x^2 - 18x + 9y^2 + 36y + 44 = 0
   \]

2. **Divide every term by 9 to simplify the equation:**
   \[
   x^2 - 2x + y^2 + 4y + \frac{44}{9} = 0
   \]

3. **Move the constant term to the other side of the equation:**
   \[
   x^2 - 2x + y^2 + 4y = -\frac{44}{9}
   \]

     \[
     x^2 - 2x + 1 - 1 + y^2 + 4y = -\frac{44}{9}
     \]

     \[
     (x - 1)^2 - 1 + y^2 + 4y = -\frac{44}{9}
     \]

5. **Complete the square for the \(y\) terms:**
   - Take the coefficient of \(y\), which is \(4\), divide it by 2 to get \(2\), and then square it to get \(4\).
   - Add and subtract \(4\) inside the equation:
     \[
     (x - 1)^2 - 1 + y^2 + 4y + 4 - 4 = -\frac{44}{9}
     \]
   - Rewrite the perfect square trinomial:
     \[
     (x - 1)^2 - 1 + (y + 2)^2 - 4 = -\frac{44}{9}
     \]

6. **Combine the constants on the left side:**
   \[
   (x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 - 5 = -\frac{44}{9}
   \]

7. **Move the constant term to the right side:**
   \[
   (x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = -\frac{44}{9} + 5
   \]

8. **Convert 5 to a fraction with a denominator of 9:**
   \[
   5 = \frac{45}{9}
   \]
   \[
   (x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = \frac{45}{9} - \frac{44}{9} = \frac{1}{9}
   \]

9. **Write the equation in the standard form:**
   \[
   (x - 1)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2

    \[
    r = \frac{1}{3}
    \]

Thus, the radius of the circle is \(\boxed{\frac{1}{3}}\).
