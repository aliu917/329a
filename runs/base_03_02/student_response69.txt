To solve the problem, we need to determine the dimensions of the rectangular picture frame and then find the sum of the lengths of the four interior edges.

1. **Identify the outer dimensions:**
   - One of the outer edges is given as 5 inches.
   - Let the other outer dimension be \( y \) inches.
   - The frame is made from one-inch-wide pieces of wood, so the inner dimensions will be \( (5-2) = 3 \) inches and \( (y-2) \) inches.

2. **Calculate the area of the outer rectangle:**
   - The area of the outer rectangle is \( 5 \times y \).

3. **Calculate the area of the inner rectangle:**
   - The area of the inner rectangle is \( 3 \times (y-2) \).

4. **Determine the area of the frame:**
   - The area of the frame is the difference between the area of the outer rectangle and the area of the inner rectangle.
   \[
   \text{Area of the frame} = 5y - 3(y-2)
   \]
   - Simplify the expression:
   \[
   5y - 3y + 6 = 2y + 6
   \]
   - We know the area of the frame is 18 square inches:
   \[
   2y + 6 = 18
   \]

5. **Solve for \( y \):**
   \[
   2y + 6 = 18
   \]
   \[
   2y = 12
   \]
   \[
   y = 6
   \]

6. **Determine the inner dimensions:**
   - One inner dimension is \( 3 \) inches.
   - The other inner dimension is \( 6 - 2 = 4 \) inches.

7. **Calculate the sum of the lengths of the four interior edges:**
   - The interior rectangle has two lengths of 3 inches and two widths of 4 inches.
   \[
   2 \times 3 + 2 \times 4 = 6 + 8 = 14
   \]

Thus, the sum of the lengths of the four interior edges of the frame is \(\boxed{14}\).
