To solve the problem, we need to determine the area of the picture given the area of the frame and the dimensions of the picture and the frame. Let's break it down step by step.

1. **Define the variables and the areas:**
   - The dimensions of the picture are \(x\) and \(y\).
   - The dimensions of the frame are \((2x + 3)\) and \((y + 2)\).
   - The area of the picture is \(A_{\text{picture}} = x \cdot y\).
   - The area of the entire framed picture is \(A_{\text{total}} = (2x + 3) \cdot (y + 2)\).
   - The area of the frame is given as 34 square inches.

2. **Set up the equation for the area of the frame:**
   \[
   A_{\text{total}} - A_{\text{picture}} = 34
   \]
   Substituting the expressions for \(A_{\text{total}}\) and \(A_{\text{picture}}\):
   \[
   (2x + 3)(y + 2) - xy = 34
   \]

3. **Expand and simplify the equation:**
   \[
   (2x + 3)(y + 2) = 2xy + 4x + 3y + 6
   \]
   Therefore:
   \[
   2xy + 4x + 3y + 6 - xy = 34
   \]
   Simplify by combining like terms:
   \[
   xy + 4x + 3y + 6 = 34
   \]
   Subtract 6 from both sides:
   \[
   xy + 4x + 3y = 28
   \]

4. **Express \(y\) in terms of \(x\):**
   To solve for \(y\), we treat the equation as a quadratic in \(y\):
   \[
   y(x + 3) + 4x = 28
   \]
   \[
   y(x + 3) = 28 - 4x
   \]
   \[
   y = \frac{28 - 4x}{x + 3}
   \]

5. **Find integer solutions for \(x\) and \(y\):**
   Since \(x\) and \(y\) are integers greater than 1, we test integer values of \(x\) to find corresponding integer values of \(y\).

   - For \(x = 2\):
     \[
     y = \frac{28 - 4(2)}{2 + 3} = \frac{28 - 8}{5} = \frac{20}{5} = 4
     \]
     This is an integer, so \(x = 2\) and \(y = 4\) is a valid solution.

6. **Calculate the area of the picture:**
   \[
   A_{\text{picture}} = x \cdot y = 2 \cdot 4 = 8
   \]

Therefore, the area of the picture is \(\boxed{8}\) square inches.
