To solve the problem, we need to determine the radius of the smaller circle given that \(PA = AB = 4\). Let's denote the radius of the smaller circle as \(r\) and the radius of the larger circle as \(R\). The distance between the centers of the two circles is \(R + r\).

First, we need to find the lengths of the segments \(PA\) and \(AB\). Since \(PA = AB = 4\), we can use the geometric properties of the tangents to the circles.

The length \(PA\) is the length of the tangent from point \(P\) to the point of tangency \(A\) on the smaller circle. Similarly, \(AB\) is the length of the tangent from point \(A\) to the point of tangency \(B\) on the larger circle. The distance between the points of tangency \(A\) and \(B\) is equal to the difference in the radii of the two circles, which is \(R - r\).

We can use the Pythagorean theorem in the right triangle formed by the radius of the smaller circle, the radius of the larger circle, and the distance between the centers of the circles. The distance between the points of tangency \(A\) and \(B\) is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs \(4\) and \(R + r\).

The distance between the centers of the circles is the sum of the radii, \(R + r\). The distance between the points of tangency \(A\) and \(B\) is the hypotenuse of a right triangle with legs \(4\) and the vertical distance between the centers of the circles, which is \(6\sqrt{2}\) (since the height of the isosceles triangle formed by the tangents and the line connecting the centers is \(6\sqrt{2}\)).

So, we have:
\[ (R - r)^2 = 4^2 + (6\sqrt{2})^2 \]
\[ (R - r)^2 = 16 + 72 \]
\[ (R - r)^2 = 88 \]
\[ R - r = \sqrt{88} = 2\sqrt{22} \]

We also know that the distance between the centers of the circles is \(R + r\), and this distance is the sum of the heights of the two circles plus the distance between the tangents, which is \(8\sqrt{2}\):
\[ R + r = 8\sqrt{2} \]

We now have a system of equations:
1. \(R - r = 2\sqrt{22}\)
2. \(R + r = 8\sqrt{2}\)

Adding these two equations, we get:
\[ 2R = 8\sqrt{2} + 2\sqrt{22} \]
\[ R = 4\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{22} \]

Subtracting the first equation from the second, we get:
\[ 2r = 8\sqrt{2} - 2\sqrt{22} \]
\[ r = 4\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{22} \]

Since \(4\sqrt{2} \approx 5.656\) and \(\sqrt{22} \approx 4.690\), we have:
\[ r \approx 4.656 - 4.690 = 0.966 \]

The area of the smaller circle is:
\[ \pi r^2 = \pi (4\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{22})^2 = \pi (32 - 16\sqrt{44} + 22) = \pi (54 - 16\sqrt{44}) \approx \pi (54 - 112.64) = \pi (2.56) = 2.56\pi \]

Thus, the area of the smaller circle is \(\boxed{2.56\pi}\).
