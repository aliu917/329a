\[ P_n = P \cdot (0.6)^n \]

\[ P \cdot (0.6)^n < 0.1 \cdot P \]

\[ (0.6)^n < 0.1 \]

\[ \ln((0.6)^n) < \ln(0.1) \]

\[ n \ln(0.6) < \ln(0.1) \]

\[ n > \frac{\ln(0.1)}{\ln(0.6)} \]

\[ \ln(0.6) \approx -0.510826 \]
\[ n > \frac{-2.302585}{-0.510826} \approx 4.506 \]

Since \( n \) must be an integer, we round up to the next whole number:

\[ n = 5 \]
