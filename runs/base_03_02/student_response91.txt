To find the perimeter of the square plot of land, we first need to determine the length of one side of the square. We know that the area of a square is given by the formula \( \text{side}^2 \). Let's denote the length of one side of the square by \( s \).

Given that the area of the square is 325 square meters, we can set up the equation:
\[
s^2 = 325
\]

To find \( s \), we take the square root of both sides of the equation:
\[
s = \sqrt{325}
\]

Next, we simplify \(\sqrt{325}\). We start by finding the prime factorization of 325:
\[
325 \div 5 = 65
\]
\[
65 \div 5 = 13
\]
\[
13 \div 13 = 1
\]

Thus, the prime factorization of 325 is:
\[
325 = 5^2 \times 13
\]

Using this factorization, we can simplify the square root:
\[
\sqrt{325} = \sqrt{5^2 \times 13} = \sqrt{5^2} \times \sqrt{13} = 5\sqrt{13}
\]

So, the length of one side of the square is \( 5\sqrt{13} \) meters.

The perimeter of a square is given by the formula:
\[
\text{Perimeter} = 4 \times \text{side}
\]

Substituting the side length we found:
\[
\text{Perimeter} = 4 \times 5\sqrt{13} = 20\sqrt{13}
\]

Therefore, the perimeter of the square plot of land is:
\[
\boxed{20\sqrt{13}}
\]
