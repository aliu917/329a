You are given a reasoning attempt for answering the following question: A company that sells widgets has to pay $\$500$ in maintenance fees each day and then it pays each worker $\$15$ per hour. Each worker makes 5 widgets per hour, which are sold at $\$3.10$ each. What is the least number of workers the company has to hire in order to make a profit in an 8-hour workday?

Attempt: To determine the least number of workers the company needs to hire to make a profit in an 8-hour workday, we need to calculate the daily costs and revenues, and find the number of workers that results in a positive profit.

1. **Calculate the daily maintenance fees:**
   \[
   \text{Daily maintenance fees} = \$500
   \]

2. **Calculate the daily cost for each worker:**
   \[
   \text{Daily cost per worker} = \text{Hourly wage} \times \text{Number of hours worked} = \$15 \times 8 = \$120
   \]

3. **Calculate the number of widgets produced by each worker in an 8-hour workday:**
   \[
   \text{Widgets per worker} = 5 \text{ widgets/hour} \times 8 \text{ hours} = 40 \text{ widgets}
   \]

4. **Calculate the revenue from selling the widgets produced by each worker:**
   \[
   \text{Revenue per worker} = \text{Price per widget} \times \text{Widgets per worker} = \$3.10 \times 40 = \$124
   \]

5. **Calculate the profit per worker:**
   \[
   \text{Profit per worker} = \text{Revenue per worker} - \text{Daily cost per worker} = \$124 - \$120 = \$4
   \]

6. **Determine the number of workers needed to cover the daily maintenance fees:**
   Let \( n \) be the number of workers.
   \[
   \text{Total profit} = n \times \text{Profit per worker} = n \times \$4
   \]
   To make a profit, the total profit must be greater than the daily maintenance fees:
   \[
   n \times \$4 > \$500
   \]
   Solving for \( n \):
   \[
   n > \frac{500}{4} = 125
   \]

Since \( n \) must be an integer, the least number of workers needed is:
\[
\boxed{126}
\]

Correct solution: We look for the least number of workers $n$ such that the cost is less than the revenue in a day of work. The cost of each worker is $\$15$ per hour, while the revenue that comes from each worker hired is $\$3.10\times5$ widgets per hour. \begin{align*}
500+8(15n)&<(8)(3.1)(5)n=124n\quad\Rightarrow\\
500+120n&<124n\quad\Rightarrow\\
500&<4n\quad\Rightarrow\\
125&<n.
\end{align*}The smallest integer greater than 125 is 126, so the company has to hire at least $\boxed{126}$ workers to make a profit.
