   \[
   \frac{8\sqrt{2}}{2} = 4\sqrt{2}
   \]

   The slant edge of the pyramid is 10 units, and the height of the pyramid can be found using the Pythagorean theorem in the right triangle formed by the height, the radius of the circumscribed circle, and the slant edge:
   \[
   h^2 + (4\sqrt{2})^2 = 10^2
   \]
   \[
   h^2 + 32 = 100
   \]
   \[
   h^2 = 68
   \]
   \[
   h = \sqrt{68} = 2\sqrt{17}
   \]

2. **Determine the height of the smaller pyramid:**

   The plane is 3 units above the base, so the height of the smaller pyramid is:
   \[
   2\sqrt{17} - 3
   \]

3. **Determine the side length of the base of the smaller pyramid:**

   Since the plane is parallel to the base, the smaller pyramid is similar to the original pyramid. The ratio of the heights of the smaller pyramid to the original pyramid is:
   \[
   \frac{2\sqrt{17} - 3}{2\sqrt{17}}
   \]

   The side length of the base of the smaller pyramid is:
   \[
   8\sqrt{2} \cdot \frac{2\sqrt{17} - 3}{2\sqrt{17}} = 8\sqrt{2} \cdot \left(1 - \frac{3}{2\sqrt{17}}\right) = 8\sqrt{2} - \frac{24\sqrt{2}}{2\sqrt{17}} = 8\sqrt{2} - \frac{12\sqrt{34}}{17}
   \]

4. **Calculate the volume of the smaller pyramid:**

   The volume \(V\) of a pyramid is given by:
   \[
   V = \frac{1}{3} \times \text{Base Area} \times \text{Height}
   \]

   The base area of the smaller pyramid is:
   \[
   \left(8\sqrt{2} - \frac{12\sqrt{34}}{17}\right)^2

   \[
   2\sqrt{17} - 3
   \]

   \[
   V = \frac{1}{3} \times \left(8\sqrt{2} - \frac{12\sqrt{34}}{17}\right)^2 \times (2\sqrt{17} - 3)
   \]

   \[
