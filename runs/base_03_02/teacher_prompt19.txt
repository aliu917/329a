Segment $DC$ is a leg of the isosceles right triangle $CDF$ whose hypotenuse is $8\sqrt{2}$.  Therefore, $CD=8\sqrt{2}/\sqrt{2}=8$.  Applying the Pythagorean theorem to triangle $ACD$ gives $AC=6$.  Since $BC=3$, this implies that $AB=3$.  By the similarity of $ABE$ and $ACD$, we find $BE=4$.   The diagonal of the smaller square is $2\cdot BE = 8$, so its area is $8^2/2=32$.
