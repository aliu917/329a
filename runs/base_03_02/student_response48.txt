To find the volume of the set of points that are inside or within one unit of a rectangular parallelepiped (box) that measures 3 by 4 by 5 units, we need to consider the volume contributions from the box itself and the surrounding regions.

1. **Volume of the box:**
   The volume of the box is:
   \[
   V_{\text{box}} = 3 \times 4 \times 5 = 60
   \]

2. **Volume of the surrounding regions:**
   These regions can be broken down into several parts:
   - The volume of the 6 faces of the box, each extended by 1 unit.
   - The volume of the 12 edges of the box, each extended by a quarter-cylinder of radius 1.
   - The volume of the 8 vertices of the box, each extended by an eighth-sphere of radius 1.

   Let's calculate each part step by step.

   **a. Volume of the 6 faces:**
   Each face of the box is extended by 1 unit, forming a prism with height 1. The total volume of these prisms is:
   \[
   V_{\text{faces}} = 2(3 \times 4 \times 1 + 3 \times 5 \times 1 + 4 \times 5 \times 1) = 2(12 + 15 + 20) = 2 \times 47 = 94
   \]

   **b. Volume of the 12 edges:**
   Each edge of the box is extended by a quarter-cylinder of radius 1 and length equal to the edge length. The volume of one quarter-cylinder is:
   \[
   V_{\text{quarter-cylinder}} = \frac{1}{4} \pi \times 1^2 \times \text{edge length}
   \]
   Summing over all 12 edges:
   \[
   V_{\text{edges}} = 4 \left( \frac{1}{4} \pi \times 1^2 \times 3 + \frac{1}{4} \pi \times 1^2 \times 4 + \frac{1}{4} \pi \times 1^2 \times 5 \right) = \pi (3 + 4 + 5) = 12\pi
   \]

   **c. Volume of the 8 vertices:**
   Each vertex is extended by an eighth-sphere of radius 1. The volume of one eighth-sphere is:
   \[
   V_{\text{eighth-sphere}} = \frac{1}{8} \times \frac{4}{3} \pi \times 1^3 = \frac{1}{6} \pi
   \]
   Summing over all 8 vertices:
   \[
   V_{\text{vertices}} = 8 \times \frac{1}{6} \pi = \frac{4}{3} \pi
   \]

3. **Total volume:**
   Adding all the components together:
   \[
   V_{\text{total}} = V_{\text{box}} + V_{\text{faces}} + V_{\text{edges}} + V_{\text{vertices}} = 60 + 94 + 12\pi + \frac{4}{3} \pi = 154 + \frac{36\pi + 4\pi}{3} = 154 + \frac{40\pi}{3}
   \]

Thus, the volume of the set is:
\[
\frac{462 + 40\pi}{3}
\]
