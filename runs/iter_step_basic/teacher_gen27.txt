Question: Let \$\mathcal{S}\$ be the set of all perfect squares whose rightmost three digits in base \$10\$ are \$256\$. Let \$\mathcal{T}\$ be the set of all numbers of the form \$\frac{x-256}{1000}\$, where \$x\$ is in \$\mathcal{S}\$. In other words, \$\mathcal{T}\$ is the set of numbers that result when the last three digits of each number in \$\mathcal{S}\$ are truncated. Find the remainder when the tenth smallest element of \$\mathcal{T}\$ is divided by \$1000\$.

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: It is apparent that for a perfect square \$s^2\$ to satisfy the constraints, we must have \$s^2 - 256 = 1000n\$ or \$(s+16)(s-16) = 1000n.\$ Now in order for \$(s+16)(s-16)\$ to be a multiple of \$1000,\$ at least one of \$s+16\$ and \$s-16\$ must be a multiple of \$5,\$ and since \$s+16 \not\equiv s-16 \pmod{5},\$ one term must have all the factors of \$5\$ and thus must be a multiple of \$125.\$ Furthermore, each of \$s+16\$ and \$s-16\$ must have at least two factors of \$2,\$ since otherwise \$(s+16)(s-16)\$ could not possibly be divisible by \$8.\$ So therefore the conditions are satisfied if either \$s+16\$ or \$s-16\$ is divisible by \$500,\$ or equivalently if \$s = 500n \pm 16.\$ Counting up from \$n=0\$ to \$n=5,\$ we see that the tenth value of \$s\$ is \$500 \cdot 5 - 16 = 2484\$ and thus the corresponding element in \$\mathcal{T}\$ is \$\frac{2484^2 - 256}{1000} = 6170 \rightarrow \boxed{170.}\$

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Define the set of perfect squares \(\mathcal{S}\) such that the rightmost three digits are \(256\).

Step 1: For a perfect square \(s^2\) to have the last three digits \(256\), we must have \(s^2 - 256 = 1000n\) for some integer \(n\).

Step 2: This can be rearranged to \((s+16)(s-16) = 1000n\).

Step 3: Since \(1000 = 2^3 \times 5^3\), at least one of the factors \((s+16)\) or \((s-16)\) must be divisible by \(5\).

Step 4: Since \(s+16 \not\equiv s-16 \pmod{5}\), one of them must be a multiple of \(125\) to satisfy the divisibility condition.

Step 5: Both factors \((s+16)\) and \((s-16)\) must individually have at least two factors of \(2\) for the product to be divisible by \(8\).

Step 6: The conditions are satisfied if either \(s+16\) or \(s-16\) is divisible by \(500\), leading to the forms \(s = 500n + 16\) or \(s = 500n - 16\).

Step 7: Count up values of \(n\) starting from \(0\) and find corresponding \(s\) values: 
- For \(n=0\), \(s = 16\)
- For \(n=1\), \(s = 484\)
- For \(n=2\), \(s = 984\)
- For \(n=3\), \(s = 1484\)
- For \(n=4\), \(s = 1984\)
- For \(n=5\), \(s = 2484\)

Step 8: The tenth value of \(s\) is \(2484\).

Step 9: Calculate the corresponding element in \(\mathcal{T}\): 
\[
\frac{2484^2 - 256}{1000}.
\]

Step 10: Calculate \(2484^2\):
\[
2484^2 = 6170256.
\]

Step 11: Subtract \(256\):
\[
6170256 - 256 = 6170000.
\]

Step 12: Divide by \(1000\):
\[
\frac{6170000}{1000} = 6170.
\]

Step 13: Find the remainder when \(6170\) is divided by \(1000\):
\[
6170 \mod 1000 = 170.
\]

Final answer: \(\boxed{170}\)
