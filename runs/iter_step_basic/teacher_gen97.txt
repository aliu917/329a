Question: Triangle \$ABC_0\$ has a right angle at \$C_0\$. Its side lengths are pairwise relatively prime positive integers, and its perimeter is \$p\$. Let \$C_1\$ be the foot of the altitude to \$\overline{AB}\$, and for \$n \geq 2\$, let \$C_n\$ be the foot of the altitude to \$\overline{C_{n-2}B}\$ in \$\triangle C_{n-2}C_{n-1}B\$. The sum \$\sum_{n=2}^\infty C_{n-2}C_{n-1} = 6p\$. Find \$p\$.

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: Do note that by counting the area in 2 ways, the first altitude is \$x = \frac{ab}{c}\$. By similar triangles, the common ratio is \$\rho = \frac{a}{c}\$ for each height, so by the geometric series formula, we have
\begin{align}
6p=\frac{x}{1-\rho} = \frac{ab}{c-a}.
\end{align}
Writing \$p=a+b+c\$ and clearing denominators, we get 13a=6p .Thus \$p=13q\$, \$a=6q\$, and \$b+c=7q\$, i.e. \$c=7q-b\$. Plugging these into \$(1)\$, we get \$78q(q-b)=6bq\$, i.e., \$14b=13q\$. Thus \$q=14r\$ and \$p=182r\$, \$b=13r\$, \$a=84r\$, \$c=85r\$. Taking \$r=1\$ (since \$a,b,c\$ are relatively prime) we get \$p=\boxed{182}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Let the side lengths of triangle \(ABC_0\) be denoted as \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\), where \(c\) is the hypotenuse and \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\) are pairwise relatively prime positive integers. Define the perimeter as \(p = a + b + c\).

Step 1: The foot of the altitude from \(C_0\) to \(\overline{AB}\) is denoted as \(C_1\) and the length of this altitude is given by the area formula: \(x = \frac{ab}{c}\).

Step 2: By using similar triangles, the ratio \(\rho\) of the heights for each subsequent foot of the altitude is found to be \(\rho = \frac{a}{c}\).

Step 3: The sum of the lengths of the segments as defined in the problem can be expressed as an infinite geometric series: \(\sum_{n=2}^\infty C_{n-2}C_{n-1} = \frac{x}{1 - \rho}\).

Step 4: Substituting \(x\) into the equation, we have \(6p = \frac{ab}{c-a}\).

Step 5: Since \(p = a + b + c\), rewrite \(p\) in terms of \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\) and clear the denominators to manipulate the equation: \(13a = 6p\).

Step 6: Express \(p\) in terms of a new variable \(q\) such that \(p = 13q\), \(a = 6q\), and \(b + c = 7q\). Let \(c = 7q - b\).

Step 7: Substitute these expressions into the earlier derived equation, resulting in \(78q(q-b) = 6bq\), simplifying to \(14b = 13q\).

Step 8: Solve for \(q\) in terms of a new variable \(r\): \(q = 14r\) leads to \(p = 182r\), \(b = 13r\), \(a = 84r\), and \(c = 85r\).

Step 9: Since \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\) must be relatively prime, set \(r = 1\).

Final answer: \(p = 182\). Thus, the final answer is \(\boxed{182}\).
