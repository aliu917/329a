Question: Let \$N\$ be the number of ways to write \$2010\$ in the form \$2010 = a_3 \cdot 10^3 + a_2 \cdot 10^2 + a_1 \cdot 10 + a_0\$, where the \$a_i\$'s are integers, and \$0 \le a_i \le 99\$. An example of such a representation is \$1\cdot 10^3 + 3\cdot 10^2 + 67\cdot 10^1 + 40\cdot 10^0\$. Find \$N\$.

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: If we choose \$a_3\$ and \$a_1\$ such that \$(10^3)(a_3) + (10)(a_1) \leq 2010\$ there is a  choice of \$a_2\$ and \$a_0\$ that makes the equality hold.  So \$N\$ is just the number of combinations of \$a_3\$ and \$a_1\$ we can pick.  If \$a_3 = 0\$ or \$a_3 = 1\$ we can let \$a_1\$ be anything from \$0\$ to \$99\$.  If \$a_3 = 2\$ then \$a_1 = 0\$ or \$a_1 = 1\$.  Thus \$N = 100 + 100 + 2 = \fbox{202}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Define the variables and the equation: We want to express \$2010\$ as \$2010 = a_3 \cdot 10^3 + a_2 \cdot 10^2 + a_1 \cdot 10 + a_0\$, where \$0 \le a_i \le 99\$ for each \$a_i\$.

Step 1: Consider the contributions of \$a_3\$ and \$a_1\$ such that \$(10^3)(a_3) + (10)(a_1) \leq 2010\$.

Step 2: Analyze the cases for \$a_3\$:
- If \$a_3 = 0\$ or \$a_3 = 1\$, then \$a_1\$ can take any of the values from \$0\$ to \$99\$.
- This gives us \$100\$ choices for \$a_1\$ when \$a_3 = 0\$ and \$100\$ choices for \$a_1\$ when \$a_3 = 1\$.

Step 3: If \$a_3 = 2\$, we can only have \$a_1 = 0\$ or \$a_1 = 1\$ to keep the sum less than or equal to \$2010\$. This gives us \$2\$ choices for \$a_1\$ when \$a_3 = 2\$.

Step 4: Sum the total number of combinations for \$N\$:
- From \$a_3 = 0\$: \$100\$ choices
- From \$a_3 = 1\$: \$100\$ choices
- From \$a_3 = 2\$: \$2\$ choices

Step 5: Calculate the total: \$N = 100 + 100 + 2 = 202\$.

Final answer: \(\boxed{202}\)
