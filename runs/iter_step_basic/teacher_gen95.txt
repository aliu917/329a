Question: Call a positive integer \$n\$ \$k\$-<i>pretty</i> if \$n\$ has exactly \$k\$ positive divisors and \$n\$ is divisible by \$k\$. For example, \$18\$ is \$6\$-pretty. Let \$S\$ be the sum of positive integers less than \$2019\$ that are \$20\$-pretty. Find \$\tfrac{S}{20}\$.

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: Every 20-pretty integer can be written in form \$n = 2^a 5^b k\$, where \$a \ge 2\$, \$b \ge 1\$, \$\gcd(k,10) = 1\$, and \$d(n) = 20\$, where \$d(n)\$ is the number of divisors of \$n\$. Thus, we have \$20 = (a+1)(b+1)d(k)\$, using the fact that the divisor function is multiplicative. As \$(a+1)(b+1)\$ must be a divisor of 20, there are not many cases to check.
If \$a+1 = 4\$, then \$b+1 = 5\$. But this leads to no solutions, as \$(a,b) = (3,4)\$ gives \$2^3 5^4 > 2019\$.
If \$a+1 = 5\$, then \$b+1 = 2\$ or \$4\$. The first case gives \$n = 2^4 \cdot 5^1 \cdot p\$ where \$p\$ is a prime other than 2 or 5. Thus we have \$80p < 2019 \implies p = 3, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23\$. The sum of all such \$n\$ is \$80(3+7+11+13+17+19+23) = 7440\$. In the second case \$b+1 = 4\$ and \$d(k) = 1\$, and there is one solution \$n = 2^4 \cdot 5^3 = 2000\$.
If \$a+1 = 10\$, then \$b+1 = 2\$, but this gives \$2^9 \cdot 5^1 > 2019\$. No other values for \$a+1\$ work.
Then we have \$\frac{S}{20} = \frac{80(3+7+11+13+17+19+23) + 2000}{20} = 372 + 100 = \boxed{472}\$.

--------------------------------------------------

Step 2: Use the formula for the number of divisors: \( d(n) = (a+1)(b+1)d(k) \).

Step 4: Check the case \( a+1 = 4 \) and \( b+1 = 5 \). This gives \( (a, b) = (3, 4) \), leading to \( n = 2^3 \cdot 5^4 = 2000 \), which is greater than 2019, so no valid solutions.

Step 5: Check the case \( a+1 = 5 \) and \( b+1 = 2 \) or \( 4 \).

Step 7: Check \( a + 1 = 10 \) and \( b + 1 = 2 \) gives \( (a, b) = (9, 1) \), leading to \( n = 2^9 \cdot 5^1 = 2560 \), which is greater than 2019.

Step 8: Conclude that the only valid \( n \) are those derived from primes \( p \) in the case \( a + 1 = 5 \) and \( b + 1 = 2 \).
