Question: Find the value of \$10\cot(\cot^{-1}3+\cot^{-1}7+\cot^{-1}13+\cot^{-1}21).\$
__TOC__

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: =
We know that \$\tan(\arctan(x)) = x\$ so we can repeatedly apply the addition formula, \$\tan(x+y) = \frac{\tan(x)+\tan(y)}{1-\tan(x)\tan(y)}\$. Let \$a = \cot^{-1}(3)\$, \$b=\cot^{-1}(7)\$, \$c=\cot^{-1}(13)\$, and \$d=\cot^{-1}(21)\$. We have
<center><p>\$\tan(a)=\frac{1}{3},\quad\tan(b)=\frac{1}{7},\quad\tan(c)=\frac{1}{13},\quad\tan(d)=\frac{1}{21}\$,</p></center>
so
<center><p>\$\tan(a+b) = \frac{\frac{1}{3}+\frac{1}{7}}{1-\frac{1}{21}} = \frac{1}{2}\$</p></center>
and
<center><p>\$\tan(c+d) = \frac{\frac{1}{13}+\frac{1}{21}}{1-\frac{1}{273}} = \frac{1}{8}\$,</p></center>
so
<center><p>\$\tan((a+b)+(c+d)) = \frac{\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{8}}{1-\frac{1}{16}} = \frac{2}{3}\$.</p></center>
Thus our answer is \$10\cdot\frac{3}{2}=\boxed{015}\$.
=

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Define variables for the angles: let \( a = \cot^{-1}(3) \), \( b = \cot^{-1}(7) \), \( c = \cot^{-1}(13) \), and \( d = \cot^{-1}(21) \).

Step 1: Recall that \( \tan(\cot^{-1}(x)) = \frac{1}{x} \). Therefore, we have:
- \( \tan(a) = \frac{1}{3} \)
- \( \tan(b) = \frac{1}{7} \)
- \( \tan(c) = \frac{1}{13} \)
- \( \tan(d) = \frac{1}{21} \)

Step 2: Use the tangent addition formula: 
\[
\tan(x+y) = \frac{\tan(x) + \tan(y)}{1 - \tan(x)\tan(y)}
\]

Step 3: Calculate \( \tan(a+b) \):
\[
\tan(a+b) = \frac{\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{7}}{1 - \frac{1}{3} \cdot \frac{1}{7}} = \frac{\frac{7 + 3}{21}}{1 - \frac{1}{21}} = \frac{\frac{10}{21}}{\frac{20}{21}} = \frac{1}{2}
\]

Step 4: Calculate \( \tan(c+d) \):
\[
\tan(c+d) = \frac{\frac{1}{13} + \frac{1}{21}}{1 - \frac{1}{13} \cdot \frac{1}{21}} = \frac{\frac{21 + 13}{273}}{1 - \frac{1}{273}} = \frac{\frac{34}{273}}{\frac{272}{273}} = \frac{34}{272} = \frac{17}{136}
\]

Step 5: Calculate \( \tan((a+b)+(c+d)) \):
\[
\tan((a+b)+(c+d)) = \frac{\frac{1}{2} + \frac{17}{136}}{1 - \frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{17}{136}} 
= \frac{\frac{68 + 17}{136}}{1 - \frac{17}{272}} 
= \frac{\frac{85}{136}}{\frac{255}{272}} = \frac{85 \cdot 272}{136 \cdot 255} = \frac{85 \cdot 2}{255} = \frac{170}{255} = \frac{34}{51} = \frac{2}{3}
\]

Step 6: Multiply the final result by 10:
\[
10 \cdot \frac{2}{3} = \frac{20}{3}
\]

Final answer: \(\boxed{6.67}\) or as a fraction, \(\boxed{\frac{20}{3}}\).
