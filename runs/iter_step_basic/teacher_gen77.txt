Question: A unicorn is tethered by a \$20\$-foot silver rope to the base of a magician's  tower whose  is \$8\$ feet. The rope is attached to the tower at ground level and to the unicorn at a height of \$4\$ feet. The unicorn has pulled the rope taut, the end of the rope is \$4\$ feet from the nearest point on the tower, and the length of the rope that is touching the tower is \$ \frac{a-\sqrt{b}}c \$ feet, where \$ a, b, \$ and \$ c \$ are  s, and \$ c \$ is prime. Find \$ a+b+c. \$

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: Note that by Power of a Point, the point the unicorn is at has power \$4 \cdot 20 = 80\$ which implies that the tangent from that point to the tower is of length \$\sqrt{80}=4\sqrt{5},\$ however this is length of the rope projected into 2-D. If we let \$\theta\$ be the angle between the horizontal and the rope, we have that \$\cos\theta=\frac{1}{5}\$ which implies that \$\sin\theta=\frac{2\sqrt{6}}{5}.\$ Note that the portion of rope not on the tower is \$4\sqrt{5} \cdot \frac{5}{2\sqrt{6}}= \frac{5\sqrt{30}}{3},\$ the requested length of rope is \$20-\frac{5\sqrt{30}}{3}=\frac{60-\sqrt{750}}{3}\$ thus the requested sum is \$\boxed{813}.\$

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Define the length of the rope as \$20\$ feet, the height of the tower as \$8\$ feet, and the height at which the rope is attached to the unicorn as \$4\$ feet. 

Step 1: Recognize that the unicorn is pulling the rope taut, making the end of the rope \$4\$ feet away from the nearest point on the tower.

Step 2: Use the Power of a Point theorem, which states that the power of the unicorn's position with respect to the tower is \$4 \cdot 20 = 80\$.

Step 3: Determine the length of the tangent from the unicorn's position to the tower, which is calculated as \$\sqrt{80} = 4\sqrt{5}\$.

Step 4: Let \$\theta\$ be the angle between the horizontal and the rope. From the geometry of the situation, we find that \$\cos\theta = \frac{1}{5}\$, thus calculating \$\sin\theta = \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{1}{5}\right)^2} = \frac{2\sqrt{6}}{5}\$.

Step 5: Calculate the portion of the rope that is not touching the tower, which is given as \$4\sqrt{5} \cdot \frac{5}{2\sqrt{6}} = \frac{5\sqrt{30}}{3}\$.

Step 6: The length of the rope that is touching the tower is then calculated as \$20 - \frac{5\sqrt{30}}{3} = \frac{60 - 5\sqrt{30}}{3}\$.

Step 7: Rewrite the expression for the length of the rope touching the tower in the form \$\frac{a - \sqrt{b}}{c}\$, identifying \$a = 60\$, \$b = 750\$, and \$c = 3\$.

Step 8: Verify that \$c\$ is prime, which it is.

Step 9: Calculate the final result as \$a + b + c = 60 + 750 + 3 = 813\$.
