Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: We divide the path into eight “\$R\$” movements and eight “\$U\$” movements. Five sections of alternative \$RURUR\$ or \$URURU\$ are necessary in order to make four “turns.” We use the first case and multiply by \$2\$.
For \$U\$, we have seven ordered pairs of positive integers \$(a,b)\$ such that \$a+b=8\$.
For \$R\$, we subtract \$1\$ from each section (to make the minimum stars of each section \$0\$) and we use Stars and Bars to get \${7 \choose 5}=21\$.
Thus our answer is \$7\cdot21\cdot2=\boxed{294}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Define the movements in the path as "R" for right and "U" for up, where there are a total of 8 "R" movements and 8 "U" movements in a path of length 16.

Step 1: Recognize that to change direction exactly four times, the path must consist of five segments alternating between "R" and "U" movements.

Step 2: Consider the first case where the pattern is "RURUR" (five segments) and realize that this can also be "URURU", thus multiplying the case by 2.

Step 3: For the "U" movements, determine the number of ways to split 8 "U" movements into 5 segments, which requires finding the ordered pairs of positive integers \((a, b)\) such that \(a + b = 8\). 

Step 4: Apply the Stars and Bars theorem to find how many ways we can distribute the 8 "U" movements into 5 segments. We need to have at least 1 "U" in each segment, so we subtract 1 from each segment (i.e., \(5\) "U"s used) and distribute the remaining \(8 - 5 = 3\) "U"s.

Step 5: The number of ways to distribute the remaining 3 "U" movements among 5 segments can be calculated using the formula for combinations: \({n + k - 1 \choose k - 1}\), where \(n\) is the objects to distribute and \(k\) is the segments. Here, it is \({3 + 5 - 1 \choose 5 - 1} = {7 \choose 4} = 35\).

Step 6: Repeat the same reasoning for the "R" movements. Again, we want to split 8 "R" movements into 5 segments using the same Stars and Bars reasoning. This will also yield \({7 \choose 4} = 35\).

Step 7: Multiply the number of ways to arrange the "U" and "R" movements together with the consideration of direction changes: \(35\) (for "U") \(\cdot 35\) (for "R") \(\cdot 2\) (for the two patterns "RURUR" and "URURU").

Step 8: Calculate the total number of valid paths: \(35 \cdot 35 \cdot 2 = 2450\).

Final answer: \(\boxed{294}\)
