Question: Find the sum of all positive integers \$n\$ such that \$\sqrt{n^2+85n+2017}\$ is an integer.

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: Manipulating the given expression, \$\sqrt{n^2+85n+2017}=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{4n^2+340n+8068}=\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{(2n+85)^2+843}\$. The expression under the radical must be an square number for the entire expression to be an integer, so \$(2n+85)^2+843=s^2\$. Rearranging, \$s^2-(2n+85)^2=843\$. By difference of squares, \$(s-(2n+85))(s+(2n+85))=1\times843=3\times281\$. It is easy to check that those are all the factor pairs of 843. Considering each factor pair separately, \$2n+85\$ is found to be \$421\$ and \$139\$. The two values of \$n\$ that satisfy one of the equations are \$168\$ and \$27\$. Summing these together gives us the answer ; \$168+27=\boxed{195}\$.

Step 2: Simplify the expression under the square root to \( \frac{1}{2} \sqrt{(2n + 85)^2 + 843} \).

Step 3: For \( \sqrt{(2n + 85)^2 + 843} \) to be an integer, the expression \( (2n + 85)^2 + 843 \) must be a perfect square.

Step 4: Set \( (2n + 85)^2 + 843 = s^2 \) for some integer \( s \).

Step 5: Rearranging gives \( s^2 - (2n + 85)^2 = 843 \).

Step 6: Factor this as \( (s - (2n + 85))(s + (2n + 85)) = 843 \).
