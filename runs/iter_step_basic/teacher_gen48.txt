Question: Two distinct, real, infinite geometric series each have a sum of \$1\$ and have the same second term. The third term of one of the series is \$1/8\$, and the second term of both series can be written in the form \$\frac{\sqrt{m}-n}p\$, where \$m\$, \$n\$, and \$p\$ are positive integers and \$m\$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find \$100m+10n+p\$.

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: Let the second term of each series be \$x\$. Then, the common ratio is \$\frac{1}{8x}\$, and the first term is \$8x^2\$.
So, the sum is \$\frac{8x^2}{1-\frac{1}{8x}}=1\$. Thus, \$64x^3-8x+1 = (4x-1)(16x^2+4x-1) = 0 \Rightarrow x = \frac{1}{4}, \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{8}\$.
The only solution in the appropriate form is \$x = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{8}\$. Therefore, \$100m+10n+p = \boxed{518}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Let the second term of each series be \(x\). The common ratio of the series is given as \(\frac{1}{8x}\), and the first term can be expressed as \(8x^2\).

Step 1: Write the formula for the sum of an infinite geometric series, which is \(\frac{a}{1 - r}\), where \(a\) is the first term and \(r\) is the common ratio.

Step 2: Substitute in the values for the first term and common ratio into the sum formula: 
\[
\frac{8x^2}{1 - \frac{1}{8x}} = 1.
\]

Step 3: Simplify the equation: 
\[
\frac{8x^2}{\frac{8x - 1}{8x}} = 1 \quad \Rightarrow \quad 8x^2 \cdot \frac{8x}{8x - 1} = 1.
\]

Step 4: Multiply both sides by \(8x - 1\) to eliminate the fraction: 
\[
8x^2 \cdot 8x = 8x - 1.
\]
This simplifies to:
\[
64x^3 - 8x + 1 = 0.
\]

Step 5: Factor the cubic equation \(64x^3 - 8x + 1\) to find its roots: 
\[
(4x - 1)(16x^2 + 4x - 1) = 0.
\]

Step 6: Find the solutions from the factored equation:
- The first factor gives \(x = \frac{1}{4}\).
- The second factor can be solved using the quadratic formula: 
\[
x = \frac{-4 \pm \sqrt{4^2 - 4 \cdot 16 \cdot (-1)}}{2 \cdot 16} = \frac{-4 \pm \sqrt{16 + 64}}{32} = \frac{-4 \pm \sqrt{80}}{32} = \frac{-4 \pm 4\sqrt{5}}{32} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{8}.
\]

Step 7: Identify the valid solution for \(x\) that can be expressed in the form \(\frac{\sqrt{m}-n}{p}\). The only relevant positive solution is:
\[
x = \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{8}.
\]

Step 8: Identify the values of \(m\), \(n\), and \(p\): Here, \(m = 5\), \(n = 1\), and \(p = 8\).

Step 9: Calculate \(100m + 10n + p\):
\[
100 \cdot 5 + 10 \cdot 1 + 8 = 500 + 10 + 8 = 518.
\]

Final answer: \(\boxed{518}\)
