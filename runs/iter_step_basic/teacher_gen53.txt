This line intersects \$\overline{CD}\$ at point \$\left(\frac{p}{q}, \frac{r}{s}\right)\$, where these fractions are in lowest terms.  What is \$p+q+r+s\$?

First, we shall find the area of quadrilateral \$ABCD\$. This can be done in any of three ways:
: \$[ABCD] = I + \dfrac{B}{2} - 1 = 5 + \dfrac{7}{2} - 1 = \dfrac{15}{2}.\$
Splitting: Drop perpendiculars from \$B\$ and \$C\$ to the x-axis to divide the quadrilateral into triangles and trapezoids, and so the area is \$1 + 5 + \dfrac{3}{2} = \dfrac{15}{2}.\$
: The area is half of \$|1 \cdot 3 - 2 \cdot 3 - 3 \cdot 4| = 15\$, or \$\dfrac{15}{2}\$.
\$[ABCD] = \frac{15}{2}\$.  Therefore, each equal piece that the line separates \$ABCD\$ into must have an area of \$\frac{15}{4}\$.
Call the point where the line through \$A\$ intersects \$\overline{CD}\$ \$E\$.  We know that \$[ADE] = \frac{15}{4} = \frac{bh}{2}\$.  Furthermore, we know that \$b = 4\$, as \$AD = 4\$.  Thus, solving for \$h\$, we find that \$2h = \frac{15}{4}\$, so \$h = \frac{15}{8}\$.  This gives that the y coordinate of E is \$\frac{15}{8}\$.
Line CD can be expressed as \$y = -3x+12\$, so the \$x\$ coordinate of E satisfies \$\frac{15}{8} = -3x + 12\$.  Solving for \$x\$, we find that \$x = \frac{27}{8}\$.
From this, we know that \$E = \left(\frac{27}{8}, \frac{15}{8}\right)\$.  \$27 + 15 + 8 + 8 = \boxed{\textbf{(B) }58}\$

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Define the coordinates of points \(A = (0, 0)\), \(B = (1, 2)\), \(C = (3, 3)\), and \(D = (4, 0)\). 

Step 1: Calculate the area of quadrilateral \(ABCD\) using the formula for the area of a polygon based on its vertices, which gives \([ABCD] = \frac{1}{2} |x_1y_2 + x_2y_3 + x_3y_4 + x_4y_1 - (y_1x_2 + y_2x_3 + y_3x_4 + y_4x_1|\).

Step 2: Substitute the coordinates into the area formula: 
\[
[ABCD] = \frac{1}{2} |0 \cdot 2 + 1 \cdot 3 + 3 \cdot 0 + 4 \cdot 0 - (0 \cdot 1 + 2 \cdot 3 + 3 \cdot 4 + 0 \cdot 0)| = \frac{1}{2} |0 + 3 + 0 + 0 - (0 + 6 + 12 + 0)| = \frac{1}{2} |3 - 18| = \frac{1}{2} \cdot 15 = \frac{15}{2}.
\]

Step 3: Since the area of quadrilateral \(ABCD\) is \(\frac{15}{2}\), the area of each piece when cut into equal areas by the line through \(A\) is \(\frac{15}{4}\).

Step 4: Let point \(E\) be where the line through \(A\) intersects \(\overline{CD}\). The area of triangle \(ADE\) is given by \([ADE] = \frac{1}{2} \cdot b \cdot h\) where \(b = AD = 4\).

Step 5: Set the area of triangle \(ADE\) equal to \(\frac{15}{4}\):
\[
\frac{1}{2} \cdot 4 \cdot h = \frac{15}{4}.
\]

Step 6: Solve for height \(h\):
\[
2h = \frac{15}{4} \implies h = \frac{15}{8}.
\]

Step 7: The y-coordinate of \(E\) is \(\frac{15}{8}\).

Step 8: Find the equation of line \(\overline{CD}\). The slope between points \(C\) and \(D\) is \(-\frac{3}{1} = -3\), and using point-slope form, the equation of line \(\overline{CD}\) is \(y = -3x + 12\).

Step 9: Substitute \(y = \frac{15}{8}\) into the line equation to find \(x\):
\[
\frac{15}{8} = -3x + 12.
\]

Step 10: Solve for \(x\):
\[
-3x = \frac{15}{8} - 12 \implies -3x = \frac{15}{8} - \frac{96}{8} \implies -3x = \frac{-81}{8} \implies x = \frac{27}{8}.
\]

Step 11: Thus, the coordinates of point \(E\) are \(\left(\frac{27}{8}, \frac{15}{8}\right)\).

Step 12: Identify \(p = 27\), \(q = 8\), \(r = 15\), \(s = 8\) and compute \(p + q + r + s\):
\[
p + q + r + s = 27 + 8 + 15 + 8 = 58.
\]

Final answer: \(\boxed{58}\)
