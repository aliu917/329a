To find the perimeter of the square plot of land given that its area is 325 square meters, we will follow these steps:

1. **Determine the side length of the square:**
   - The area \( A \) of a square is given by the formula \( A = s^2 \), where \( s \) is the side length of the square.
   - Given \( A = 325 \) square meters, we can set up the equation:
     \[
     s^2 = 325
     \]
   - To find \( s \), we take the square root of both sides:
     \[
     s = \sqrt{325}
     \]

2. **Simplify the square root:**
   - We need to simplify \( \sqrt{325} \). First, we find the prime factorization of 325:
     \[
     325 = 5 \times 65 = 5 \times 5 \times 13 = 5^2 \times 13
     \]
   - Using the property of square roots, \( \sqrt{a \times b} = \sqrt{a} \times \sqrt{b} \), we can simplify:
     \[
     \sqrt{325} = \sqrt{5^2 \times 13} = \sqrt{5^2} \times \sqrt{13} = 5\sqrt{13}
     \]

3. **Calculate the perimeter of the square:**
   - The perimeter \( P \) of a square is given by the formula \( P = 4s \).
   - Substituting \( s = 5\sqrt{13} \) into the formula:
     \[
     P = 4 \times 5\sqrt{13} = 20\sqrt{13}
     \]

Therefore, the perimeter of the square plot of land is \(\boxed{20\sqrt{13}}\) meters.
