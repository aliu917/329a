To find the product of the area and perimeter of square \(ABCD\) on the 5 by 5 grid, we need to first determine the side length of the square. The vertices of the square are \(A(3,4)\), \(B(4,1)\), \(C(1,0)\), and \(D(0,3)\).

\[
AB = \sqrt{(4-3)^2 + (1-4)^2} = \sqrt{1^2 + (-3)^2} = \sqrt{1 + 9} = \sqrt{10}
\]
Since \(ABCD\) is a square, all sides are equal, so the side length of the square is \(\sqrt{10}\) cm.

Next, we calculate the area of the square. The area \(A\) of a square with side length \(s\) is given by:
\[
A = s^2 = (\sqrt{10})^2 = 10 \text{ cm}^2
\]

Then, we calculate the perimeter of the square. The perimeter \(P\) of a square with side length \(s\) is given by:
\[
P = 4s = 4\sqrt{10} \text{ cm}
\]

Now, we find the product of the area and the perimeter:
\[
\text{Product} = A \times P = 10 \times 4\sqrt{10} = 40\sqrt{10}
\]

Thus, the product of the area of square \(ABCD\) and the perimeter of square \(ABCD\) is \(\boxed{40\sqrt{10}}\).
