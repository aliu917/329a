To find the ratio of nine to thirteen and round it to the nearest tenth, follow these steps:

1. **Set up the ratio as a division problem**: The ratio of nine to thirteen can be expressed as a fraction: \(\frac{9}{13}\).

2. **Perform the division**: Divide 9 by 13.
   \[
   9 \div 13 \approx 0.6923
   \]

3. **Round to the nearest tenth**: Look at the digit in the hundredths place (2 in this case) to decide whether to round up or down. Since 2 is less than 5, you round down.
   \[
   0.6923 \approx 0.7
   \]

4. **Write the final answer**: Place the rounded number in the required format.
   \[
   \boxed{0.7}
   \]
