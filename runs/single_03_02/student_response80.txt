To solve the given problem, we start by simplifying the left-hand side of the equation:

\[
\frac{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2}{\left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{5}\right)^2}
\]

First, we calculate the squares of the fractions:

\[
\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4}, \quad \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{9}, \quad \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{16}, \quad \left(\frac{1}{5}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{25}
\]

Next, we substitute these values back into the expression:

\[
\frac{\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{9}}{\frac{1}{16} + \frac{1}{25}}
\]

To add the fractions in the numerator and the denominator, we find a common denominator. For the numerator, the common denominator of 4 and 9 is 36:

\[
\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{9} = \frac{9}{36} + \frac{4}{36} = \frac{13}{36}
\]

For the denominator, the common denominator of 16 and 25 is 400:

\[
\frac{1}{16} + \frac{1}{25} = \frac{25}{400} + \frac{16}{400} = \frac{41}{400}
\]

Now we substitute these back into the original expression:

\[
\frac{\frac{13}{36}}{\frac{41}{400}} = \frac{13}{36} \times \frac{400}{41} = \frac{13 \times 400}{36 \times 41} = \frac{5200}{1476}
\]

We simplify the fraction \(\frac{5200}{1476}\) by finding the greatest common divisor (GCD) of 5200 and 1476. The GCD is 4, so we divide both the numerator and the denominator by 4:

\[
\frac{5200 \div 4}{1476 \div 4} = \frac{1300}{369}
\]

We check if \(\frac{1300}{369}\) can be simplified further. Since 1300 and 369 have no common factors other than 1, the fraction is in its simplest form.

We are given that this expression equals \(\frac{13x}{41y}\):

\[
\frac{1300}{369} = \frac{13x}{41y}
\]

By comparing the numerators and denominators, we see that:

\[
1300 = 13x \quad \text{and} \quad 369 = 41y
\]

Solving for \(x\) and \(y\):

\[
x = \frac{1300}{13} = 100, \quad y = \frac{369}{41} = 9
\]

Now, we need to find \(\sqrt{x} \div \sqrt{y}\):

\[
\sqrt{x} \div \sqrt{y} = \sqrt{100} \div \sqrt{9} = \frac{\sqrt{100}}{\sqrt{9}} = \frac{10}{3}
\]

Thus, the final answer is:

\[
\boxed{\frac{10}{3}}
\]
