You are given a reasoning attempt for answering the following question: Every June 1, an ecologist takes a census of the number of wrens in a state park. She noticed that the number is decreasing by $40\%$ each year. If this trend continues, in what year will the census show that the number of wrens is less than $10\%$ of what it was on June 1, 2004?

Attempt: To determine in which year the number of wrens will be less than 10% of what it was on June 1, 2004, we need to model the population decline using the given annual decrease rate of 40%.

1. **Define the initial population and the decay factor:**
   - Let \( P_0 \) be the initial population of wrens on June 1, 2004.
   - The population decreases by 40% each year, which means it retains 60% of its value each year.
   - Therefore, the decay factor is \( 0.60 \).

2. **Formulate the population after \( n \) years:**
   - The population after \( n \) years can be expressed as:
     \[
     P_n = P_0 \times (0.60)^n
     \]

3. **Set up the inequality to find when the population is less than 10% of the initial population:**
   - We want \( P_n < 0.10 \times P_0 \).
   - Substituting the expression for \( P_n \):
     \[
     P_0 \times (0.60)^n < 0.10 \times P_0
     \]

4. **Simplify the inequality:**
   - Divide both sides by \( P_0 \) (assuming \( P_0 \neq 0 \)):
     \[
     (0.60)^n < 0.10
     \]

5. **Solve for \( n \) using logarithms:**
   - Take the natural logarithm of both sides:
     \[
     \ln((0.60)^n) < \ln(0.10)
     \]
   - Use the property of logarithms \( \ln(a^b) = b \ln(a) \):
     \[
     n \ln(0.60) < \ln(0.10)
     \]
   - Since \( \ln(0.60) \) is negative, divide both sides by \( \ln(0.60) \) and reverse the inequality:
     \[
     n > \frac{\ln(0.10)}{\ln(0.60)}
     \]

6. **Calculate the value:**
   - Compute \( \ln(0.10) \) and \( \ln(0.60) \):
     \[
     \ln(0.10) \approx -2.302585
     \]
     \[
     \ln(0.60) \approx -0.510826
     \]
   - Divide to find \( n \):
     \[
     n > \frac{-2.302585}{-0.510826} \approx 4.506
     \]

7. **Determine the smallest integer \( n \) greater than 4.506:**
   - The smallest integer greater than 4.506 is 5.

8. **Find the corresponding year:**
   - Since \( n = 5 \) represents the number of years after 2004, the year is:
     \[
     2004 + 5 = 2009
     \]

Therefore, the census will show that the number of wrens is less than 10% of what it was on June 1, 2004, in the year \(\boxed{2009}\).
