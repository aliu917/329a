To find the minimum of the function \( f(x, y) = \frac{xy}{x^2 + y^2} \) in the domain \(\frac{2}{5} \le x \le \frac{1}{2}\) and \(\frac{1}{3} \le y \le \frac{3}{8}\), we will follow these steps:

1. **Evaluate the function at the boundary points of the domain.**
2. **Determine if there are any critical points inside the domain.**
3. **Compare the values obtained to find the minimum.**

### Step 1: Evaluate the function at the boundary points

The boundary points are:
- \( x = \frac{2}{5} \)
- \( x = \frac{1}{2} \)
- \( y = \frac{1}{3} \)
- \( y = \frac{3}{8} \)

#### Case 1: \( x = \frac{2}{5} \)
- \( y = \frac{1}{3} \): 
  \[
  f\left(\frac{2}{5}, \frac{1}{3}\right) = \frac{\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)}{\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2} = \frac{\frac{2}{15}}{\frac{4}{25} + \frac{1}{9}} = \frac{\frac{2}{15}}{\frac{36}{225} + \frac{25}{225}} = \frac{\frac{2}{15}}{\frac{61}{225}} = \frac{2 \cdot 225}{15 \cdot 61} = \frac{30}{61}
  \]

- \( y = \frac{3}{8} \):
  \[
  f\left(\frac{2}{5}, \frac{3}{8}\right) = \frac{\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)\left(\frac{3}{8}\right)}{\left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{3}{8}\right)^2} = \frac{\frac{6}{40}}{\frac{4}{25} + \frac{9}{64}} = \frac{\frac{3}{20}}{\frac{256}{1600} + \frac{225}{1600}} = \frac{\frac{3}{20}}{\frac{481}{1600}} = \frac{3 \cdot 1600}{20 \cdot 481} = \frac{240}{481}
  \]

#### Case 2: \( x = \frac{1}{2} \)
- \( y = \frac{1}{3} \):
  \[
  f\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{3}\right) = \frac{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)}{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2} = \frac{\frac{1}{6}}{\frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{9}} = \frac{\frac{1}{6}}{\frac{9}{36} + \frac{4}{36}} = \frac{\frac{1}{6}}{\frac{13}{36}} = \frac{1 \cdot 36}{6 \cdot 13} = \frac{6}{13}
  \]

- \( y = \frac{3}{8} \):
  \[
  f\left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{8}\right) = \frac{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)\left(\frac{3}{8}\right)}{\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{3}{8}\right)^2} = \frac{\frac{3}{16}}{\frac{1}{4} + \frac{9}{64}} = \frac{\frac{3}{16}}{\frac{16}{64} + \frac{9}{64}} = \frac{\frac{3}{16}}{\frac{25}{64}} = \frac{3 \cdot 64}{16 \cdot 25} = \frac{12}{25}
  \]

### Step 2: Determine if there are any critical points inside the domain

To find critical points, we need to solve the system of equations given by the partial derivatives set to zero:
\[
\frac{\partial f}{\partial x} = \frac{(x^2 + y^2) \cdot y - xy \cdot 2x}{(x^2 + y^2)^2} = 0
\]
\[
\frac{\partial f}{\partial y} = \frac{(x^2 + y^2) \cdot x - xy \cdot 2y}{(x^2 + y^2)^2} = 0
\]

Simplifying these, we get:
\[
(x^2 + y^2) y - 2x^2 y = 0 \implies y(x^2 + y^2 - 2x^2) = 0 \implies y(y - x^2) = 0
\]
\[
(x^2 + y^2) x - 2xy^2 = 0 \implies x(x^2 + y^2 - 2y^2) = 0 \implies x(x^2 - y^2) = 0
\]

From these, we have:
- \( y = 0 \) or \( y = x^2 \)
- \( x = 0 \) or \( x = y \)

Given the domain constraints, \( x \) and \( y \) cannot be zero. Therefore, we consider:
- \( y = x^2 \)
- \( x = y \)

Substituting \( y = x^2 \) into the function:
\[
f(x, x^2) = \frac{x \cdot x^2}{x^2 + (x^2)^2} = \frac{x^3}{x^2 + x^4} = \frac{x^3}{x^2(1 + x^2)} = \frac{x}{1 + x^2}
\]

Substituting \( x = y \) into the function:
\[
f(x, x) = \frac{x \cdot x}{x^2 + x^2} = \frac{x^2}{2x^2} = \frac{1}{2}
\]

### Step 3: Compare the values obtained

The values we have are:
- \( \frac{30}{61} \approx 0.4918 \)
- \( \frac{240}{481} \approx 0.5 \)
- \( \frac{6}{13} \approx 0.4615 \)
- \( \frac{12}{25} = 0.48 \)
- \( \frac{x}{1 + x^2} \) for \( x \) in \(\left[\frac{2}{5}, \frac{1}{2}\right]\)

To find the minimum value of \( \frac{x}{1 + x^2} \) in this interval, we take the derivative and set it to zero:
\[
\frac{d}{dx} \left( \frac{x}{1 + x^2} \right) = \frac{(1 + x^2) - x \cdot 2x}{(1 + x^2)^2} = \frac{1 - x^2}{(1 + x^2)^2}
\]
Setting the derivative to zero:
\[
1 - x^2 = 0 \implies x = 1 \text{ or } x = -1
\]

Since \( x \) is in \(\left[\frac{2}{5}, \frac{1}{2}\right]\), we evaluate at the endpoints:
\[
\frac{\frac{2}{5}}{1 + \left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^2} = \frac{\frac{2}{5}}{1 + \frac{4}{25}} = \frac{\frac{2}{5}}{\frac{29}{25}} = \frac{2 \cdot 25}{5 \cdot 29} = \frac{10}{29} \approx 0.3448
\]
\[
\frac{\frac{1}{2}}{1 + \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{1 + \frac{1}{4}} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}}{\frac{5}{4}} = \frac{1 \cdot 4}{2 \cdot 5} = \frac{2}{5} = 0.4
\]

The minimum value is \( \frac{10}{29} \).
