To solve this problem, we need to determine the value of \( m \) such that the probability that the two mathematicians meet is 40%. We will use geometric probability to find this value.

1. **Define the problem in a coordinate system:**
   Let \( X \) and \( Y \) be the times (in minutes past 9 a.m.) that the two mathematicians arrive at the cafeteria. Both \( X \) and \( Y \) are uniformly distributed between 0 and 60. The condition that they meet is \( |X - Y| \leq m \).

2. **Visualize the problem:**
   We can represent the possible arrival times of the two mathematicians on a 60 by 60 square in the coordinate plane. The area of this square is \( 60 \times 60 = 3600 \).

3. **Determine the area where they do not meet:**
   The area where they do not meet is given by the two regions where \( X - Y > m \) and \( Y - X > m \). These regions are two right triangles, each with legs of length \( 60 - m \). The area of each triangle is \( \frac{1}{2} (60 - m)^2 \). Therefore, the total area where they do not meet is \( (60 - m)^2 \).

4. **Calculate the area where they meet:**
   The area where they meet is the total area of the square minus the area where they do not meet:
   \[
   3600 - (60 - m)^2
   \]
   The probability that they meet is the ratio of the area where they meet to the total area of the square:
   \[
   \frac{3600 - (60 - m)^2}{3600} = 0.40
   \]
   Simplify the equation:
   \[
   3600 - (60 - m)^2 = 1440
   \]
   \[
   (60 - m)^2 = 2160
   \]
   \[
   60 - m = \sqrt{2160} = 12\sqrt{15}
   \]
   \[
   m = 60 - 12\sqrt{15}
   \]
   Here, \( a = 60 \), \( b = 12 \), and \( c = 15 \). Therefore, \( a + b + c = 60 + 12 + 15 = 87 \).

The final answer is:
\[
\boxed{87}
