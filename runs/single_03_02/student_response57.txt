To solve for \( m \) in the given sequence, we start by analyzing the recurrence relation:
\[ a_{k+1} = a_{k-1} - \frac{3}{a_k} \]
for \( k = 1, 2, \dots, m-1 \). We are given the initial conditions \( a_0 = 37 \), \( a_1 = 72 \), and \( a_m = 0 \).

First, we multiply both sides of the recurrence relation by \( a_k \):
\[ a_k a_{k+1} = a_k a_{k-1} - 3. \]
This can be rewritten as:
\[ a_k a_{k+1} - a_k a_{k-1} = -3. \]

Next, we sum this equation from \( k = 1 \) to \( k = m-1 \):
\[ \sum_{k=1}^{m-1} (a_k a_{k+1} - a_k a_{k-1}) = \sum_{k=1}^{m-1} (-3). \]
The left-hand side is a telescoping series:
\[ (a_1 a_2 - a_0 a_1) + (a_2 a_3 - a_1 a_2) + \cdots + (a_{m-1} a_m - a_{m-2} a_{m-1}) = a_{m-1} a_m - a_0 a_1. \]
Since \( a_m = 0 \), the left-hand side simplifies to:
\[ -a_0 a_1. \]
The right-hand side is:
\[ \sum_{k=1}^{m-1} (-3) = -3(m-1). \]
Thus, we have:
\[ -a_0 a_1 = -3(m-1). \]
Substituting the given values \( a_0 = 37 \) and \( a_1 = 72 \), we get:
\[ -37 \cdot 72 = -3(m-1). \]
Simplifying, we find:
\[ 37 \cdot 72 = 3(m-1). \]
\[ 3(m-1) = 2664. \]
\[ m-1 = 888. \]
\[ m = 889. \]

Therefore, the value of \( m \) is \(\boxed{889}\).
