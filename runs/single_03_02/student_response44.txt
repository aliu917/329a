To solve the problem, we need to find the point \( (x, 0) \) where a line tangent to both circles intersects the \( x \)-axis. Let's start by visualizing the situation. We have two circles with centers at \((0,0)\) and \((15,0)\) and radii 2 and 7, respectively. The line is tangent to both circles and intersects the \( x \)-axis at some point \( (x,0) \).

First, we note that the distance between the centers of the two circles is 15 units. If a line is tangent to both circles, the distance from the center of each circle to the point of tangency is equal to the radius of the respective circle. The line is also perpendicular to the radii at the points of tangency.

Consider the right triangle formed by the line from the origin to the point of tangency on the first circle, the line from the point of tangency to the point where the line intersects the \( x \)-axis, and the segment along the \( x \)-axis from the origin to the point of intersection. The same applies to the second circle.

Let's denote the point of tangency on the first circle as \( T_1 \) and on the second circle as \( T_2 \). The line is tangent to the first circle at \( T_1 \) and to the second circle at \( T_2 \). The distance from the center of the first circle to the point of tangency is 2, and the distance from the center of the second circle to the point of tangency is 7. The distance between the centers of the circles is 15.

If we draw a line from the point of tangency on the first circle to the point of tangency on the second circle, it will be parallel to the \( x \)-axis and will be a horizontal distance of \( x - 2 \) from the center of the first circle and \( x - 15 - 7 = x - 22 \) from the center of the second circle. The length of this horizontal line segment is the same as the distance between the points of tangency, which is the same as the distance between the centers of the circles minus the radii of the two circles, i.e., \( 15 - 2 - 7 = 6 \).

Now, we can use similar triangles to find \( x \). The triangles formed by the radii and the segments from the centers to the \( x \)-axis are similar. The ratio of the corresponding sides of these triangles is the same. The ratio of the distance from the center of the first circle to the point of intersection of the tangent line with the \( x \)-axis to the distance from the center of the second circle to the point of intersection is the same as the ratio of the radii of the circles to the distance between the centers minus the radii.

This gives us the equation:
\[
\frac{x}{15 - x} = \frac{2}{7}
\]
Cross-multiplying gives:
\[
7x = 2(15 - x)
\]
\[
7x = 30 - 2x
\]
\[
9x = 30
\]
\[
x = \frac{30}{9} = \frac{10}{3}
\]

Thus, the value of \( x \) is \(\boxed{\frac{10}{3}}\).
