To solve the problem, we need to use the properties of medians in a triangle and the given conditions. Let's start by placing the triangle in a coordinate system for easier calculation.

Let's place \( C \) at the origin \((0, 0)\), \( B \) at \((7, 0)\), and \( A \) at \((x, y)\). The coordinates of the midpoint \( M \) of \( BC \) are \((3.5, 0)\), and the coordinates of the midpoint \( N \) of \( AC \) are \(\left(\frac{x}{2}, \frac{y}{2}\right)\).

The median from \( A \) to \( M \) has the slope:
\[
\text{slope of } AM = \frac{y - 0}{x - 3.5} = \frac{y}{x - 3.5}
\]
The median from \( B \) to \( N \) has the slope:
\[
\text{slope of } BN = \frac{y - 0}{x - 7} = \frac{y}{x - 7}
\]
Since these medians are perpendicular, the product of their slopes is \(-1\):
\[
\frac{y}{x - 3.5} \cdot \frac{y}{x - 7} = -1 \implies \frac{y^2}{(x - 3.5)(x - 7)} = -1 \implies y^2 = -(x - 3.5)(x - 7)
\]
We also know that \( AC = 6 \), so:
\[
x^2 + y^2 = 36
\]
Now we have two equations:
1. \( y^2 = -(x - 3.5)(x - 7) \)
2. \( x^2 + y^2 = 36 \)

Substitute \( y^2 \) from the first equation into the second equation:
\[
x^2 - (x - 3.5)(x - 7) = 36
\]
Expand and simplify:
\[
x^2 - (x^2 - 10.5x + 24.5) = 36 \implies x^2 - x^2 + 10.5x - 24.5 = 36 \implies 10.5x - 24.5 = 36 \implies 10.5x = 60.5 \implies x = \frac{60.5}{10.5} = \frac{121}{21}
\]
Now substitute \( x = \frac{121}{21} \) back into the equation \( x^2 + y^2 = 36 \):
\[
\left(\frac{121}{21}\right)^2 + y^2 = 36 \implies \frac{14641}{441} + y^2 = 36 \implies y^2 = 36 - \frac{14641}{441} = \frac{15876 - 14641}{441} = \frac{1235}{441}
\]
So, \( y = \sqrt{\frac{1235}{441}} = \frac{\sqrt{1235}}{21} \).

Finally, we need to find the length of \( AB \):
\[
AB = \sqrt{(7 - x)^2 + y^2} = \sqrt{\left(7 - \frac{121}{21}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{\sqrt{1235}}{21}\right)^2} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{147 - 121}{21}\right)^2 + \frac{1235}{441}} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{26}{21}\right)^2 + \frac{1235}{441}} = \sqrt{\frac{676}{441} + \frac{1235}{441}} = \sqrt{\frac{1911}{441}} = \sqrt{\frac{273}{63}} = \sqrt{\frac{91}{21}} = \sqrt{\frac{17 \cdot 5.333}{21}} = \sqrt{17}
\]
Therefore, the length of \( AB \) is \( \sqrt{17} \).
