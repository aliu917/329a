To find the perimeter of the square plot of land, we first need to determine the length of one side of the square. We know that the area of a square is given by the formula:

\[ \text{Area} = \text{side}^2 \]

Given that the area of the square plot is 325 square meters, we can set up the equation:

\[ \text{side}^2 = 325 \]

To find the length of one side, we take the square root of both sides of the equation:

\[ \text{side} = \sqrt{325} \]

Next, we need to simplify \(\sqrt{325}\). We do this by finding the prime factorization of 325:

\[ 325 = 5 \times 65 = 5 \times 5 \times 13 = 5^2 \times 13 \]

So, we can rewrite \(\sqrt{325}\) as:

\[ \sqrt{325} = \sqrt{5^2 \times 13} = \sqrt{5^2} \times \sqrt{13} = 5\sqrt{13} \]

Therefore, the length of one side of the square is \(5\sqrt{13}\) meters.

The perimeter of a square is given by the formula:

\[ \text{Perimeter} = 4 \times \text{side} \]

Substituting the length of one side, we get:

\[ \text{Perimeter} = 4 \times 5\sqrt{13} = 20\sqrt{13} \]
