To solve the problem, we need to determine the volume of the smaller pyramid formed when a plane parallel to the base of the original pyramid cuts it 3 units above the base.

#### Step 1: Determine the Height of the Original Pyramid
We start by finding the height of the original pyramid. The base of the pyramid is a square with side length \(8\sqrt{2}\) units. The center of the base is the midpoint of the square, which is at a distance of \(4\sqrt{2}\) units from any side of the base.

The slant edge of the pyramid is 10 units. We use the Pythagorean theorem in the right triangle formed by the height of the pyramid, half the diagonal of the base, and the slant edge.

1. Calculate the distance from the center of the base to a vertex of the base:
   \[
   \text{Diagonal of the base} = 8\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{2} = 16
   \]
   \[
   \text{Distance from center to vertex} = \frac{16}{2} = 8
   \]

2. Use the Pythagorean theorem:
   \[
   h^2 + (8\sqrt{2})^2 = 10^2
   \]
   \[
   h^2 + 128 = 100
   \]
   \[
   h^2 = 100 - 128
   \]
   \[
   h^2 = -28 \quad \text{(This is incorrect, let's recheck the setup)}
   \]

We need to use the correct setup:
   \[
   h^2 + 32 = 100
   \]
   \[
   h^2 = 64
   \]
   \[
   h = 8
   \]

#### Step 2: Determine the Height of the Smaller Pyramid
The plane is 3 units above the base. Therefore, the height of the smaller pyramid is:
   \[
   h_{\text{small}} = 8 - 3 = 5 \text{ units}
   \]

#### Step 3: Determine the Side Length of the Base of the Smaller Pyramid
The smaller pyramid is similar to the original pyramid. The ratio of the heights of the smaller pyramid to the original pyramid is:
   \[
   \frac{5}{8}
   \]

Since the base of the smaller pyramid is similar to the base of the original pyramid, the side length of the base of the smaller pyramid is:
   \[
   8\sqrt{2} \times \frac{5}{8} = 5\sqrt{2} \text{ units}
   \]

#### Step 4: Calculate the Volume of the Smaller Pyramid
The volume \(V\) of a pyramid is given by:
   \[
   V = \frac{1}{3} \times \text{Base Area} \times \text{Height}
   \]

1. Calculate the base area of the smaller pyramid:
   \[
   \text{Base Area} = (5\sqrt{2})^2 = 50
   \]

2. Calculate the volume:
   \[
   V = \frac{1}{3} \times 50 \times 5 = \frac{250}{3} \text{ cubic units}
   \]

Thus, the volume of the new pyramid is:
\[
\boxed{\frac{250}{3}}
