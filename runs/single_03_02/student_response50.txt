To find the radius of the circle given by the equation \(9x^2 - 18x + 9y^2 + 36y + 44 = 0\), we need to rewrite the equation in the standard form of a circle's equation, \((x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2\). Let's go through the steps:

1. **Group the \(x\) and \(y\) terms:**
   \[
   9x^2 - 18x + 9y^2 + 36y + 44 = 0
   \]
   Group the \(x\) terms and the \(y\) terms:
   \[
   9(x^2 - 2x) + 9(y^2 + 4y) + 44 = 0
   \]

2. **Factor out the common factor from the grouped terms:**
   \[
   9(x^2 - 2x) + 9(y^2 + 4y) + 44 = 0
   \]
   Factor out 9 from both grouped terms:
   \[
   9(x^2 - 2x + y^2 + 4y) + 44 = 0
   \]

3. **Complete the square for the \(x\) terms:**
   \[
   x^2 - 2x
   \]
   To complete the square, add and subtract \((\frac{-2}{2})^2 = 1\):
   \[
   x^2 - 2x + 1 - 1 = (x-1)^2 - 1
   \]

4. **Complete the square for the \(y\) terms:**
   \[
   y^2 + 4y
   \]
   To complete the square, add and subtract \((\frac{4}{2})^2 = 4\):
   \[
   y^2 + 4y + 4 - 4 = (y+2)^2 - 4
   \]

5. **Substitute the completed squares back into the equation:**
   \[
   9((x-1)^2 - 1 + (y+2)^2 - 4) + 44 = 0
   \]
   Simplify inside the parentheses:
   \[
   9((x-1)^2 + (y+2)^2 - 5) + 44 = 0
   \]
   Distribute the 9:
   \[
   9(x-1)^2 + 9(y+2)^2 - 45 + 44 = 0
   \]
   Combine the constants:
   \[
   9(x-1)^2 + 9(y+2)^2 - 1 = 0
   \]

6. **Isolate the squared terms:**
   \[
   9(x-1)^2 + 9(y+2)^2 = 1
   \]
   Divide every term by 9 to get the standard form:
   \[
   (x-1)^2 + (y+2)^2 = \frac{1}{9}
   \]

7. **Identify the radius:**
   The equation \((x-1)^2 + (y+2)^2 = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^2\) is in the standard form \((x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2\), where \(r = \frac{1}{3}\).

Thus, the radius of the circle is \(\boxed{\frac{1}{3}}\).
