To determine the area and perimeter of square \(ABCD\), we first need to find the side length of the square. The coordinates of the vertices \(A\), \(B\), \(C\), and \(D\) are given as follows:
- \(A = (3, 4)\)
- \(B = (4, 1)\)
- \(C = (1, 0)\)
- \(D = (0, 3)\)

We can use the distance formula to find the side length of the square. The distance formula between two points \((x_1, y_1)\) and \((x_2, y_2)\) is:
\[ d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2} \]

First, we calculate the distance \(AB\):
\[ AB = \sqrt{(4 - 3)^2 + (1 - 4)^2} = \sqrt{1^2 + (-3)^2} = \sqrt{1 + 9} = \sqrt{10} \]

Since \(ABCD\) is a square, all sides are equal. Therefore, the side length of the square is \(\sqrt{10}\) cm.

Next, we calculate the area of the square. The area \(A\) of a square with side length \(s\) is given by:
\[ A = s^2 \]
Substituting \(s = \sqrt{10}\):
\[ A = (\sqrt{10})^2 = 10 \, \text{cm}^2 \]

Now, we calculate the perimeter of the square. The perimeter \(P\) of a square with side length \(s\) is given by:
\[ P = 4s \]
Substituting \(s = \sqrt{10}\):
\[ P = 4 \sqrt{10} \, \text{cm} \]

Finally, we find the product of the area and the perimeter:
\[ \text{Product} = A \times P = 10 \times 4\sqrt{10} = 40\sqrt{10} \]

Thus, the final answer is:
\[
\boxed{40\sqrt{10}}
