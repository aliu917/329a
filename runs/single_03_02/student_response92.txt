To find the area of the hexagon \(AB'CA'BC'\), we start by understanding the geometric properties and relationships in the given triangle \(ABC\) and its circumcircle.

1. **Identify Key Points and Properties:**
   - The points \(A'\), \(B'\), and \(C'\) are the intersections of the perpendicular bisectors of the sides \(BC\), \(CA\), and \(AB\) with the circumcircle of \(\triangle ABC\).
   - The perpendicular bisector of a side of a triangle passes through the circumcenter and is also the angle bisector of the arc opposite that side.
   - The points \(A'\), \(B'\), and \(C'\) are the reflections of the vertices \(A\), \(B\), and \(C\) across the opposite sides, respectively.

2. **Perimeter and Circumradius:**
   - The perimeter of \(\triangle ABC\) is 35.
   - The radius \(R\) of the circumcircle is 8.

3. **Area of the Hexagon:**
   - The hexagon \(AB'CA'BC'\) can be divided into six smaller triangles: \(\triangle OAB'\), \(\triangle OBC'\), \(\triangle OCA'\), \(\triangle OBA\), \(\triangle OCB\), and \(\triangle OCA\), where \(O\) is the circumcenter.
   - Each of these triangles has a base as a side of \(\triangle ABC\) and a height equal to the radius \(R\) of the circumcircle.

4. **Area of Each Triangle:**
   - The area of each triangle \(\triangle OAB'\) (or any similar triangle) is \(\frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times R\).
   - For \(\triangle OAB'\), the base is \(AB\).
   - The total area of the hexagon is the sum of the areas of these six triangles.

5. **Sum of the Areas:**
   - The total area of the hexagon is:
     \[
     \text{Area} = 3 \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base of } \triangle OAB \times R \right) + 3 \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base of } \triangle OAB' \times R \right)
     \]
   - Since the bases \(AB\), \(BC\), and \(CA\) are the sides of \(\triangle ABC\) and the total perimeter is 35, we have:
     \[
     \text{Area} = 3 \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \times \text{side} \times R \right) \times 3
     \]
   - Simplifying, we get:
     \[
     \text{Area} = 3 \times \left( \frac{1}{2} \times 35 \times 8 \right) = 3 \times 140 = 420
     \]

Thus, the area of the hexagon \(AB'CA'BC'\) is \(\boxed{420}\).
