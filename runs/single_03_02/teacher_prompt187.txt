A plane parallel to the base of the cone divides the cone into two solids, a smaller cone-shaped solid $C$ and a frustum-shaped solid $F,$ in such a way that the ratio between the areas of the painted surfaces of $C$ and $F$ and the ratio between the volumes of $C$ and $F$ are both equal to $k$. Given that $k=\frac m n,$ where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers, find $m+n.$


Attempt: To solve the problem, we need to find the ratio \( k \) such that the ratio of the painted surfaces and the ratio of the volumes of the smaller cone \( C \) and the frustum \( F \) are both equal to \( k \).

The ratio of the heights of the smaller cone to the original cone is \( \frac{h}{4} \), and the ratio of the radii is also \( \frac{r}{3} \). Therefore, \( r = \frac{3h}{4} \).

### Step 1: Calculate the volume of the smaller cone \( C \)
The volume \( V_C \) of the smaller cone is given by:
\[
V_C = \frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3} \pi \left( \frac{3h}{4} \right)^2 h = \frac{1}{3} \pi \frac{9h^2}{16} h = \frac{3\pi h^3}{16}

The volume \( V_F \) of the frustum is the volume of the original cone minus the volume of the smaller cone:
\[
V_F = \frac{1}{3} \pi (3)^2 (4) - \frac{3\pi h^3}{16} = 12\pi - \frac{3\pi h^3}{16} = \frac{192\pi - 3\pi h^3}{16} = \frac{3\pi (64 - h^3)}{16}

Since the ratio of the volumes is \( k \):
\[
\frac{V_C}{V_F} = k \implies \frac{\frac{3\pi h^3}{16}}{\frac{3\pi (64 - h^3)}{16}} = k \implies \frac{h^3}{64 - h^3} = k

The lateral surface area \( A_L(C) \) of the smaller cone is:
\[

The total surface area \( A(C) \) of the smaller cone is:
\[

A(F) = A_L(F) + A_B(F) + A_T(F) = \pi \left( 3 + \frac{3h}{4} \right) \sqrt{32 - 8h + h^2} + 9\pi + \pi \frac{9h^2}{16}
