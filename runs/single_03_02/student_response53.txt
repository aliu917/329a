To determine the least number of workers the company needs to hire to make a profit in an 8-hour workday, we need to calculate both the total costs and the total revenue generated by the workers. Let's break this down step by step.

1. **Calculate the total cost:**
   - The company incurs a daily maintenance fee of \$500.
   - Each worker is paid \$15 per hour and works 8 hours a day, so each worker costs \(15 \times 8 = \$120\) per day.
   - If the company hires \(n\) workers, the total daily labor cost is \(120n\).

   Therefore, the total daily cost is:
   \[
   \text{Total cost} = 500 + 120n
   \]

2. **Calculate the total revenue:**
   - Each worker produces 5 widgets per hour and works 8 hours a day, so each worker produces \(5 \times 8 = 40\) widgets per day.
   - If the company hires \(n\) workers, the total number of widgets produced per day is \(40n\).
   - Each widget is sold for \$3.10, so the total daily revenue is \(3.10 \times 40n = 124n\).

3. **Set up the inequality for profitability:**
   - For the company to make a profit, the total revenue must be greater than the total cost.
   - Therefore, we set up the inequality:
   \[
   124n > 500 + 120n
   \]

4. **Solve the inequality:**
   - Subtract \(120n\) from both sides:
   \[
   124n - 120n > 500
   \]
   \[
   4n > 500
   \]
   - Divide both sides by 4:
   \[
   n > 125
   \]

Since \(n\) must be a whole number (you can't hire a fraction of a worker), we round up to the next whole number. Therefore, the least number of workers the company needs to hire to make a profit is:
\[
n = 126
\]

Thus, the least number of workers the company has to hire to make a profit in an 8-hour workday is \(\boxed{126}\).
