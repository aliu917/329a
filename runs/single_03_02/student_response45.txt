To solve for the value of \(x\) where the line tangent to both circles intersects the \(x\)-axis, we start by visualizing the setup. The first circle has a radius of 2 units and is centered at \((0, 0)\), and the second circle has a radius of 7 units and is centered at \((15, 0)\).

The line tangent to both circles will touch each circle at a point, and the radius at the point of tangency will be perpendicular to the tangent line. This forms two right triangles, one from the center of the first circle to the point of tangency and the tangent line, and another from the center of the second circle to the point of tangency and the tangent line.

Let the point of tangency on the first circle be \((a, 2)\) and on the second circle be \((15 - b, 7)\). The tangent line intersects the \(x\)-axis at \((x, 0)\). The horizontal distances from the centers of the circles to the point of tangency are \(a\) and \(15 - b\), respectively. Since the tangent line is the same, the vertical distances from the point of tangency to the \(x\)-axis are the radii of the circles, 2 and 7, respectively.

We can use similar triangles to set up the following proportion:
\[
\frac{2}{a} = \frac{7}{15 - b}
\]
Since the tangent line is the same, the horizontal distance from the center of the first circle to the point of tangency is \(a\) and the horizontal distance from the center of the second circle to the point of tangency is \(15 - b\). The total horizontal distance between the centers of the circles is \(15\), so \(a + (15 - b) = 15\), which simplifies to \(a = b\).

Now, substituting \(b = a\) into the proportion:
\[
\frac{2}{a} = \frac{7}{15 - a}
\]
Cross-multiplying gives:
\[
2(15 - a) = 7a
\]
Expanding and solving for \(a\):
\[
30 - 2a = 7a \implies 30 = 9a \implies a = \frac{30}{9} = \frac{10}{3}
\]
So, the point of tangency on the first circle is \(\left(\frac{10}{3}, 2\right)\) and on the second circle is \(\left(15 - \frac{10}{3}, 7\right) = \left(\frac{35}{3}, 7\right)\).

The slope of the tangent line can be found using the points \(\left(\frac{10}{3}, 2\right)\) and \(\left(\frac{35}{3}, 7\right)\):
\[
\text{slope} = \frac{7 - 2}{\frac{35}{3} - \frac{10}{3}} = \frac{5}{\frac{25}{3}} = \frac{5 \cdot 3}{25} = \frac{3}{5}
\]
The equation of the tangent line in point-slope form using the point \(\left(\frac{10}{3}, 2\right)\) is:
\[
y - 2 = \frac{3}{5} \left(x - \frac{10}{3}\right)
\]
Setting \(y = 0\) to find the \(x\)-intercept:
\[
0 - 2 = \frac{3}{5} \left(x - \frac{10}{3}\right) \implies -2 = \frac{3}{5} x - 2 \implies 0 = \frac{3}{5} x \implies x = \frac{10}{3} + \frac{30}{15} = \frac{30}{5} = \frac{35}{3} - \frac{10}{3} = \frac{25}{3}
\]
Thus, the value of \(x\) is:
\[
\boxed{\frac{25}{3}}
\]
