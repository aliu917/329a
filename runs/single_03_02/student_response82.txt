To find the remainder of \(19^{1999}\) when divided by 25, we can use Euler's theorem. Euler's theorem states that if \(a\) and \(n\) are coprime, then \(a^{\phi(n)} \equiv 1 \pmod{n}\), where \(\phi\) is the Euler's totient function. Here, \(a = 19\) and \(n = 25\). Since 19 and 25 are coprime, we can apply Euler's theorem.

First, calculate \(\phi(25)\):
\[
\phi(25) = 25 \left(1 - \frac{1}{5}\right) = 25 \cdot \frac{4}{5} = 20
\]
So, by Euler's theorem:
\[
19^{20} \equiv 1 \pmod{25}
\]
We need to express \(19^{1999}\) in terms of \(19^{20}\):
\[
19^{1999} = 19^{20 \cdot 99 + 19} = (19^{20})^{99} \cdot 19^{19} \equiv 1^{99} \cdot 19^{19} \equiv 19^{19} \pmod{25}
\]
Now, we need to find \(19^{19} \pmod{25}\). We can use successive squaring to simplify this calculation.

First, calculate \(19^2 \pmod{25}\):
\[
19^2 = 361 \equiv 361 - 14 \cdot 25 = 361 - 350 = 11 \pmod{25}
\]
Next, calculate \(19^4 \pmod{25}\):
\[
19^4 = (19^2)^2 \equiv 11^2 = 121 \equiv 121 - 4 \cdot 25 = 121 - 100 = 21 \pmod{25}
\]
Next, calculate \(19^8 \pmod{25}\):
\[
19^8 = (19^4)^2 \equiv 21^2 = 441 \equiv 441 - 17 \cdot 25 = 441 - 425 = 16 \pmod{25}
\]
Next, calculate \(19^{16} \pmod{25}\):
\[
19^{16} = (19^8)^2 \equiv 16^2 = 256 \equiv 256 - 10 \cdot 25 = 256 - 250 = 6 \pmod{25}
\]
Now, we can express \(19^{19}\) as \(19^{16} \cdot 19^2 \cdot 19\):
\[
19^{19} \equiv 6 \cdot 11 \cdot 19 \pmod{25}
\]
Calculate \(6 \cdot 11 \pmod{25}\):
\[
6 \cdot 11 = 66 \equiv 66 - 2 \cdot 25 = 66 - 50 = 16 \pmod{25}
\]
Now, calculate \(16 \cdot 19 \pmod{25}\):
\[
16 \cdot 19 = 304 \equiv 304 - 12 \cdot 25 = 304 - 300 = 4 \pmod{25}
\]
Therefore, the remainder of \(19^{1999}\) when divided by 25 is \(\boxed{4}\).
