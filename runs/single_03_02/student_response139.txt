Let's denote the outer dimensions of the rectangular frame as \(5\) inches by \(y\) inches. Since the frame is made from one-inch-wide pieces of wood, the inner dimensions of the frame will be \(5 - 2 = 3\) inches by \(y - 2\) inches. The area of the frame itself is the area of the outer rectangle minus the area of the inner rectangle. We are given that the area of the frame is \(18\) square inches.

First, we calculate the area of the outer rectangle:
\[
5 \times y = 5y \text{ square inches}
\]

Next, we calculate the area of the inner rectangle:
\[
3 \times (y - 2) = 3y - 6 \text{ square inches}
\]

The area of the frame is the difference between the area of the outer rectangle and the area of the inner rectangle:
\[
5y - (3y - 6) = 5y - 3y + 6 = 2y + 6
\]

We know that the area of the frame is \(18\) square inches, so we set up the equation:
\[
2y + 6 = 18
\]

To find \(y\), we solve the equation:
\[
2y + 6 - 6 = 18 - 6 \implies 2y = 12 \implies y = 6
\]

So, the outer dimensions of the frame are \(5\) inches by \(6\) inches, and the inner dimensions are \(3\) inches by \(4\) inches. The sum of the lengths of the four interior edges of the frame is:
\[
2 \times 3 + 2 \times 4 = 6 + 8 = 14 \text{ inches}
\]

Thus, the sum of the lengths of the four interior edges of the frame is \(\boxed{14}\).
