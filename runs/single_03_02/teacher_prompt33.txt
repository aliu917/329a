You are given a reasoning attempt for answering the following question: The solid $S$ consists of the set of all points $(x,y,z)$ such that $|x| + |y| \le 1,$ $|x| + |z| \le 1,$ and $|y| + |z| \le 1.$  Find the volume of $S.$

Attempt: To find the volume of the solid \( S \) defined by the inequalities \( |x| + |y| \le 1 \), \( |x| + |z| \le 1 \), and \( |y| + |z| \le 1 \), we need to understand the shape and symmetry of the solid.

### Step 1: Understanding the Inequalities
Each inequality represents a region in 3D space bounded by planes. For example, \( |x| + |y| \le 1 \) describes a square-based pyramid (or a diamond shape in the \(xy\)-plane) when \( z = 0 \). Similarly, \( |x| + |z| \le 1 \) and \( |y| + |z| \le 1 \) describe analogous regions in the \(xz\)- and \(yz\)-planes, respectively.

### Step 2: Symmetry and Octants
The solid is symmetric with respect to all three coordinate planes. Therefore, we can consider the volume in just one octant and then multiply by 8 to get the total volume.

### Step 3: Volume in One Octant
Consider the first octant where \( x, y, z \ge 0 \). In this octant, the inequalities simplify to:
\[ x + y \le 1, \quad x + z \le 1, \quad y + z \le 1. \]

### Step 4: Visualizing the Region in One Octant
The region in the first octant is a tetrahedron with vertices at \((0,0,0)\), \((1,0,0)\), \((0,1,0)\), and \((0,0,1)\).

### Step 5: Calculating the Volume of the Tetrahedron
The volume \( V \) of a tetrahedron with vertices \((0,0,0)\), \((a,0,0)\), \((0,b,0)\), and \((0,0,c)\) is given by:
\[ V = \frac{1}{6} \times \text{base area} \times \text{height}. \]
Here, the base is a right triangle in the \(xy\)-plane with vertices \((0,0)\), \((1,0)\), and \((0,1)\), and the height is 1 (along the \(z\)-axis).

The area of the base triangle is:
\[ \text{Base area} = \frac{1}{2} \times 1 \times 1 = \frac{1}{2}. \]

Thus, the volume of the tetrahedron is:
\[ V = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{1}{2} \times 1 = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{6}. \]

### Step 6: Total Volume
Since the solid is symmetric in all eight octants, the total volume \( V_{\text{total}} \) is:
\[ V_{\text{total}} = 8 \times \frac{1}{6} = \frac{8}{6} = \frac{4}{3}. \]

Thus, the volume of the solid \( S \) is:
\[
\boxed{\frac{4}{3}}
\]

Correct solution: By symmetry, we can focus on the octant where $x,$ $y,$ $z$ are all positive.  In this octant, the condition $|x| + |y| = 1$ becomes $x + y = 1,$ which is the equation of a plane.  Hence, the set of points in this octant such that $|x| + |y| \le 1$ is the set of points bound by the plane $x + y = 1,$ $x = 0,$ and $y = 0.$

One pyramid has vertices $(0,0,0),$ $(1,0,0),$ $(0,1,0),$ and $\left( \frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{2} \right).$  The volume of this pyramid is
