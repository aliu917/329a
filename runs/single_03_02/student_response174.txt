To determine how many positive divisors of 3240 are multiples of 3, we will follow these steps:

1. **Find the prime factorization of 3240.**
2. **Determine the total number of divisors of 3240.**
3. **Find the number of divisors of 3240 that are not multiples of 3.**
4. **Subtract the number of non-multiples of 3 from the total number of divisors to get the number of divisors that are multiples of 3.**

**Step 1: Prime factorization of 3240**

First, we divide 3240 by the smallest prime numbers:

\[
3240 \div 2 = 1620
\]
\[
1620 \div 2 = 810
\]
\[
810 \div 2 = 405
\]

405 is not divisible by 2, so we move to the next smallest prime number, which is 3:

\[
405 \div 3 = 135
\]
\[
135 \div 3 = 45
\]
\[
45 \div 3 = 15
\]
\[
15 \div 3 = 5
\]

5 is a prime number. Therefore, the prime factorization of 3240 is:

\[
3240 = 2^3 \times 3^4 \times 5
\]

**Step 2: Total number of divisors of 3240**

The total number of divisors of a number \( n = p_1^{e_1} \times p_2^{e_2} \times \cdots \times p_k^{e_k} \) is given by:

\[
(e_1 + 1)(e_2 + 1) \cdots (e_k + 1)
\]

For \( 3240 = 2^3 \times 3^4 \times 5^1 \):

\[
(3 + 1)(4 + 1)(1 + 1) = 4 \times 5 \times 2 = 40
\]

So, 3240 has 40 divisors in total.

**Step 3: Number of divisors of 3240 that are not multiples of 3**

To find the number of divisors that are not multiples of 3, we consider the prime factorization without the factor of 3:

\[
3240 = 2^3 \times 3^4 \times 5
\]

Without the factor of 3, the remaining factorization is:

\[
2^3 \times 5
\]

The number of divisors of \( 2^3 \times 5 \) is:

\[
(3 + 1)(1 + 1) = 4 \times 2 = 8
\]

So, there are 8 divisors of 3240 that are not multiples of 3.

**Step 4: Number of divisors of 3240 that are multiples of 3**

Subtract the number of non-multiples of 3 from the total number of divisors:

\[
40 - 8 = 32
\]

Therefore, the number of positive divisors of 3240 that are multiples of 3 is:

\[
\boxed{32}
