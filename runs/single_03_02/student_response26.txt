To find \( s(1) \), we need to understand the relationship between the functions \( t(x) \) and \( s(t(x)) \). Given:
\[ t(x) = 3x - 8 \]
\[ s(t(x)) = x^2 + 3x - 2 \]

First, we need to determine the value of \( x \) such that \( t(x) = 1 \).

1. **Solve for \( x \) in \( t(x) = 1 \):**
   \[
   t(x) = 3x - 8 = 1
   \]
   \[
   3x - 8 = 1
   \]
   \[
   3x = 9
   \]
   \[
   x = 3
   \]

2. **Substitute \( x = 3 \) into \( s(t(x)) \):**
   Since \( t(3) = 1 \), we have:
   \[
   s(t(3)) = s(1)
   \]
   Given:
   \[
   s(t(x)) = x^2 + 3x - 2
   \]
   Substitute \( x = 3 \):
   \[
   s(t(3)) = 3^2 + 3 \cdot 3 - 2
   \]
   \[
   s(1) = 9 + 9 - 2
   \]
   \[
   s(1) = 16
   \]

Thus, the value of \( s(1) \) is \(\boxed{16}\).
