To find the ratio of nine to thirteen and round it to the nearest tenth, we will follow these steps:

1. **Set up the ratio as a division problem:**
   \[
   \frac{9}{13}
   \]

2. **Perform the division:**
   \[
   9 \div 13 \approx 0.6923
   \]

3. **Round the result to the nearest tenth:**
   - The tenths place is the first digit to the right of the decimal point, which is 6.
   - The hundredths place (second digit to the right of the decimal point) is 9, which is greater than or equal to 5.
   - According to rounding rules, if the digit in the hundredths place is 5 or greater, we round up the tenths place by 1.

   Therefore, rounding 0.6923 to the nearest tenth gives us:
   \[
   0.7
   \]

4. **Present the final answer:**
   \[
   \boxed{0.7}
   \]
