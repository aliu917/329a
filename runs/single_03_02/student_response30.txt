To find the area of the picture, we first need to determine the dimensions \(x\) and \(y\) of the picture. The area of the frame is given as 34 square inches. The area of the frame is the area of the entire framed picture minus the area of the picture itself.

The dimensions of the entire framed picture are \((2x + 3)\) and \((y + 2)\). Therefore, the area of the entire framed picture is:
\[
(2x + 3)(y + 2)
\]
The area of the picture is:
\[
xy
\]
The area of the frame is the difference between the area of the entire framed picture and the area of the picture:
\[
(2x + 3)(y + 2) - xy = 34
\]
Expanding the left side, we get:
\[
2xy + 4x + 3y + 6 - xy = 34
\]
Simplifying, we have:
\[
xy + 4x + 3y + 6 = 34
\]
Subtracting 6 from both sides, we get:
\[
xy + 4x + 3y = 28
\]
We need to find integer values of \(x\) and \(y\) that satisfy this equation. We can try different values for \(x\) and solve for \(y\).

Let's try \(x = 2\):
\[
2y + 4(2) + 3y = 28 \implies 2y + 8 + 3y = 28 \implies 5y + 8 = 28 \implies 5y = 20 \implies y = 4
\]
So, \(x = 2\) and \(y = 4\) is a solution. Let's verify:
\[
(2(2) + 3)(4 + 2) - 2 \cdot 4 = 7 \cdot 6 - 8 = 42 - 8 = 34
\]

\[
xy = 2 \cdot 4 = 8
