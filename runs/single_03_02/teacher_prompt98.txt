Let $p\,$ be the probability that, after a suitable rotation, a brick of dimensions $a_1 \times a_2 \times a_3\,$ can be enclosed in a box of dimensions $b_1 \times b_2 \times b_3\,$, with the sides of the brick parallel to the sides of the box. \[
\binom{1000}{6}

\[
p = \frac{\binom{1000}{6}}{\binom{1000}{6} \cdot 720 / 6!} = \frac{\binom{1000}{6}}{\binom{1000}{6}} = \frac{1}{20}
\]

Thus, the probability \(p\) is \(\frac{1}{20}\). The sum of the numerator and the denominator is:
