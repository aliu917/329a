\[ (4a + 2b + c) - (a + b + c) = 3 - (-1), \]

\[ (9a - 3b + c) - (4a + 2b + c) = 4 - 3, \]
\[ 5a - 5b = 1, \]

Now we have a new system of two equations:

Add these two equations to eliminate \( b \):
\[ (3a + b) + (a - b) = 4 + \frac{1}{5}, \]
\[ 4a = \frac{20}{5} + \frac{1}{5}, \]
\[ 4a = \frac{21}{5}, \]
