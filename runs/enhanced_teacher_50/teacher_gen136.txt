Question: In right \$\triangle ABC\$ with hypotenuse \$\overline{AB}\$, \$AC = 12\$, \$BC = 35\$, and \$\overline{CD}\$ is the altitude to \$\overline{AB}\$. Let \$\omega\$ be the circle having \$\overline{CD}\$ as a diameter. Let \$I\$ be a point outside \$\triangle ABC\$ such that \$\overline{AI}\$ and \$\overline{BI}\$ are both tangent to circle \$\omega\$. The ratio of the perimeter of \$\triangle ABI\$ to the length \$AB\$ can be expressed in the form \$\frac {m}{n}\$, where \$m\$ and \$n\$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find \$m + n\$.

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: First, note that \$AB=37\$; let the tangents from \$I\$ to \$\omega\$ have length \$x\$. Then the perimeter of \$\triangle ABI\$ is equal to 2(x+AD+DB)=2(x+37). It remains to compute \$\dfrac{2(x+37)}{37}=2+\dfrac{2}{37}x\$.
Observe \$CD=\dfrac{12\cdot 35}{37}=\dfrac{420}{37}\$, so the radius of \$\omega\$ is \$\dfrac{210}{37}\$. We may also compute \$AD=\dfrac{12^{2}}{37}\$ and \$DB=\dfrac{35^{2}}{37}\$ by similar triangles. Let \$O\$ be the center of \$\omega\$; notice that \tan(\angle DAO)=\dfrac{DO}{AD}=\dfrac{210/37}{144/37}=\dfrac{35}{24} so it follows \sin(\angle DAO)=\dfrac{35}{\sqrt{35^{2}+24^{2}}}=\dfrac{35}{\sqrt{1801}} while \$\cos(\angle DAO)=\dfrac{24}{\sqrt{1801}}\$. By the double-angle formula \$\sin(2\theta)=2\sin\theta\cos\theta\$, it turns out that \sin(\angle BAI)=\sin(2\angle DAO)=\dfrac{2\cdot 35\cdot 24}{1801}=\dfrac{1680}{1801}
Using the area formula \$\dfrac{1}{2}ab\sin(C)\$ in \$\triangle ABI\$, [ABI]=\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(\dfrac{144}{37}+x\right)(37)\left(\dfrac{1680}{1801}\right)=\left(\dfrac{840}{1801}\right)(144+37x). But also, using \$rs\$, [ABI]=\left(\dfrac{210}{37}\right)(37+x). Now we can get \dfrac{[ABI]}{210}=\dfrac{4(144+37x)}{1801}=\dfrac{37+x}{37} so multiplying everything by \$37\cdot 1801=66637\$ lets us solve for \$x\$: 21312+5476x=66637+1801x. We have \$x=\dfrac{66637-21312}{5476-1801}=\dfrac{45325}{3675}=\dfrac{37}{3}\$, and now 2+\dfrac{2}{37}x=2+\dfrac{2}{3}=\dfrac{8}{3} giving the answer, \$\boxed{011}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Note that the hypotenuse \( AB = 37 \) from the Pythagorean theorem: \( AB = \sqrt{AC^2 + BC^2} = \sqrt{12^2 + 35^2} = \sqrt{144 + 1225} = \sqrt{1369} = 37 \).

Step 1: Let the tangents from point \( I \) to circle \( \omega \) have length \( x \).

Step 2: The perimeter of triangle \( \triangle ABI \) is equal to \( 2(x + AD + DB) = 2(x + 37) \).

Step 3: Compute the ratio of the perimeter of \( \triangle ABI \) to the length \( AB \): 
\[
\frac{2(x + 37)}{37} = 2 + \frac{2}{37}x.
\]

Step 4: Find the altitude \( CD \) using the area formula: 
\[
CD = \frac{AC \cdot BC}{AB} = \frac{12 \cdot 35}{37} = \frac{420}{37}.
\]

Step 5: The radius of circle \( \omega \) is \( \frac{CD}{2} = \frac{210}{37} \).

Step 6: Compute \( AD \) and \( DB \) using similar triangles:
\[
AD = \frac{12^2}{37} = \frac{144}{37}, \quad DB = \frac{35^2}{37} = \frac{1225}{37}.
\]

Step 7: Let \( O \) be the center of circle \( \omega \). Use \( \tan(\angle DAO) = \frac{DO}{AD} = \frac{210/37}{144/37} = \frac{210}{144} = \frac{35}{24} \).

Step 8: Calculate \( \sin(\angle DAO) \) and \( \cos(\angle DAO) \):
\[
\sin(\angle DAO) = \frac{35}{\sqrt{35^2 + 24^2}} = \frac{35}{\sqrt{1801}}, \quad \cos(\angle DAO) = \frac{24}{\sqrt{1801}}.
\]

Step 9: Use the double-angle formula to find \( \sin(\angle BAI) \):
\[
\sin(2\angle DAO) = 2 \sin(\angle DAO) \cos(\angle DAO) = 2 \cdot \frac{35}{\sqrt{1801}} \cdot \frac{24}{\sqrt{1801}} = \frac{1680}{1801}.
\]

Step 10: Calculate the area \( [ABI] \) using the area formula for triangles:
\[
[ABI] = \frac{1}{2} \left(\frac{144}{37} + x\right)(37) \left(\frac{1680}{1801}\right) = \left(\frac{840}{1801}\right)(144 + 37x).
\]

Step 11: Also, using the formula \( rs \):
\[
[ABI] = \left(\frac{210}{37}\right)(37 + x).
\]

Step 12: Set the two expressions for the area equal and simplify:
\[
\frac{840(144 + 37x)}{1801} = \frac{210(37 + x)}{37}.
\]

Step 13: Multiply both sides by \( 37 \cdot 1801 = 66637 \) to eliminate the fractions:
\[
21312 + 5476x = 66637 + 1801x.
\]

Step 14: Solve for \( x \):
\[
5476x - 1801x = 66637 - 21312 \implies 3675x = 45325 \implies x = \frac{45325}{3675} = \frac{37}{3}.
\]

Step 15: Substitute \( x \) back into the perimeter ratio:
\[
2 + \frac{2}{37}x = 2 + \frac{2}{37} \cdot \frac{37}{3} = 2 + \frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{3}.
\]

Final answer: \(\boxed{011}\)
