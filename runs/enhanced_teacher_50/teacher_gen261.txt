Question: Tom, Dorothy, and Sammy went on a vacation and agreed to split the costs evenly.  During their trip Tom paid \$105, Dorothy paid \$125, and Sammy paid \$175.  In order to share costs equally, Tom gave Sammy \$t\$ dollars, and Dorothy gave Sammy \$d\$ dollars.  What is \$t-d\$?
\$ \textbf{(A)}\ 15\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 20\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 25\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ 30\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 35 \$

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: The total amount paid is \$105 + 125 + 175 = 405\$.  To get how much each should have paid, we do \$405/3 = 135\$.
Thus, we know that Tom needs to give Sammy 30 dollars, and Dorothy 10 dollars.  This means that \$t-d = 30 - 10 = \boxed{\textbf{(B) }20}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Calculate the total amount paid by Tom, Dorothy, and Sammy, which is \$105 + 125 + 175\$.

Step 1: Determine how much each person should have paid by dividing the total amount by 3: \$405 / 3 = 135\$.

Step 2: Calculate how much Tom needs to give to Sammy: \$135 - 105 = 30\$.

Step 3: Calculate how much Dorothy needs to give to Sammy: \$135 - 125 = 10\$.

Step 4: Now, find the difference \$t - d\$, where \$t\$ is the amount Tom gives to Sammy and \$d\$ is the amount Dorothy gives to Sammy: \$t - d = 30 - 10\$.

Final answer: \(\boxed{20}\)
