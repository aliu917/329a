Step 2: Calculate the height of triangle AEF. The height \( h' \) is given by \( h' = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \times EF = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} x \).
Step 3: Express the length of segment DB in terms of \( x \): \( DB = h - h' = 420\sqrt{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}x \).
Step 4: Express the length of segment BE in terms of \( x \): \( BE = AB - AE = 840 - x \).
Step 5: Use the property of angle DBC being 90 degrees and angle DBE being 30 degrees to find the area of triangle BED: 
\[
\text{Area of } \triangle BED = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (\sin 30) \cdot (420\sqrt{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}x) \cdot (840 - x).
\]
Step 6: Use the property of angle AFG being 120 degrees to find the area of triangle AFG:
\[
\text{Area of } \triangle AFG = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (\sin 120) \cdot (x^2).

Step 7: Set up the equation relating the areas of triangles AFG and BED based on the given ratio \( \frac{\text{Area of } \triangle AFG}{\text{Area of } \triangle BED} = \frac{8}{9} \):
\[
\frac{\frac{1}{2} \cdot (\sin 120) \cdot x^2}{\frac{1}{2} \cdot (\sin 30) \cdot (420\sqrt{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}x) \cdot (840 - x)} = \frac{8}{9}.

\[
\frac{x^2}{\left(420 - \frac{x}{2}\right)(840 - x)} = \frac{8}{9}.
\]
Step 9: Scale down the equation by 420:
\[
\frac{x^2}{\left(1 - \frac{x}{2}\right)(2 - x)} = \frac{8}{9}.

Step 10: Cross-multiply and simplify:
\[
8\left(1 - \frac{x}{2}\right)(2 - x) = 9x^2.

Step 11: Expand and rearrange the equation:
\[
16 - 16x + 4x^2 = 9x^2 \implies 5x^2 + 16x - 16 = 0.

Step 12: Factor the quadratic equation:
\[
(5x - 4)(x + 4) = 0.
\]
Step 13: Solve for \( x \) and discard the negative solution:
\[
x = \frac{4}{5} \text{ (valid)}, \quad x = -4 \text{ (not valid)}.
\]
Step 14: Calculate the actual value of \( AF \) by scaling back up:
\[
AF = \frac{4}{5} \cdot 420 = 336.
