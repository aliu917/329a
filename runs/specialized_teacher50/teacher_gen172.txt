CORRECT SOLUTION STEPS:

Step 1: Identify the equation for the diagonal segment represented by the coordinates \( (x, y, z) = m(a, b, c) \) where \( m \in \mathbb{R}, 0 < m \le 1 \).
Step 2: Consider a point on the diagonal with coordinates \( (ma, mb, mc) \).
Step 3: Make key observations about when the point crosses the boundaries of the \( 1 \times 1 \times 1 \) cubes:
- When exactly one of \( ma, mb, mc \) becomes a positive integer, the point crosses a face of a cube.
- When exactly two of \( ma, mb, mc \) become positive integers, the point crosses an edge of a cube.
- When all three \( ma, mb, mc \) become positive integers, the point enters a cube through a vertex.
Step 4: The total number of cubes the diagonal passes is equal to the number of points on the diagonal with one or more positive integers as coordinates.
Step 5: Calculate the cuts through the \( x \)-planes defined by \( x=1,2,3,\ldots,a \), which results in \( a \) cuts.
Step 6: Apply similar reasoning for the \( y \)-planes and \( z \)-planes, yielding \( b \) cuts and \( c \) cuts respectively.
Step 7: The total initial count of cube passes is \( a + b + c \), but we must account for over-counting.
Step 8: Identify over-counting when 2 or more coordinates are integers. For each pair, the diagonal passes through the cube twice instead of once. The number of such points is given by \( \gcd(a,b) + \gcd(b,c) + \gcd(c,a) \).
Step 9: Adjust for points where all three coordinates are integers, which are over-counted three times. The correction term is \( \gcd(a,b,c) \).
Step 10: Formulate the final equation for the total diagonal cube passes:
\[
D = a + b + c - \left[ \gcd(a,b) + \gcd(b,c) + \gcd(c,a) \right] + \gcd(a,b,c)
\]
Step 11: Calculate \( \gcd(150, 324) = 6 \), \( \gcd(324, 375) = 3 \), \( \gcd(150, 375) = 75 \), and \( \gcd(150, 324, 375) = 3 \).
Step 12: Substitute these values into the equation for \( D \):
\[
D = 150 + 324 + 375 - (6 + 3 + 75) + 3
\]
Step 13: Simplify the calculation:
\[
D = 150 + 324 + 375 - 84 + 3
\]
Step 14: Compute the final total to find \( D \):
\[
D = 150 + 324 + 375 - 84 + 3 = 768
\]

Final answer: \(\boxed{768}\)
