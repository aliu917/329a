Question: Let \$N\$ be the number of complex numbers \$z\$ with the properties that \$|z|=1\$ and \$z^{6!}-z^{5!}\$ is a real number. Find the remainder when \$N\$ is divided by \$1000\$.

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: Let \$a=z^{120}\$. This simplifies the problem constraint to \$a^6-a \in \mathbb{R}\$. This is true if \$\text{Im}(a^6)=\text{Im}(a)\$. Let \$\theta\$ be the angle \$a\$ makes with the positive x-axis. Note that there is exactly one \$a\$ for each angle \$0\le\theta<2\pi\$. We are given \$\sin\theta = \sin{6\theta}\$. Note that \$\sin \theta = \sin (\pi - \theta)\$ and \$\sin \theta = \sin (\theta + 2\pi)\$. We can use these facts to create two types of solutions:
\sin \theta = \sin ((2m + 1)\pi - \theta)
which implies that \$(2m+1)\pi-\theta = 6\theta\$ and reduces to \$\frac{(2m + 1)\pi}{7} = \theta\$. There are 7 solutions for this.
\sin \theta = \sin (2n\pi + \theta)
which implies that \$2n\pi+\theta=6\theta\$ and reduces to \$\frac{2n\pi}{5} = \theta\$. There are 5 solutions for this, totaling 12 values of \$a\$.
For each of these solutions for \$a\$, there are necessarily \$120\$ solutions for \$z\$. Thus, there are \$12\cdot 120=1440\$ solutions for \$z\$, yielding an answer of \$\boxed{440}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Let \( a = z^{120} \). This converts the original problem into a simpler form.
Step 1: The condition \( z^{6!} - z^{5!} \) being real means \( a^6 - a \in \mathbb{R} \).
Step 2: This condition holds if \( \text{Im}(a^6) = \text{Im}(a) \).
Step 3: Let \( \theta \) be the angle \( a \) makes with the positive x-axis, then \( a = e^{i\theta} \).
Step 4: From the condition \( \sin \theta = \sin 6\theta \), we deduce that this can happen in two cases.
Step 5: The first case is \( \sin \theta = \sin((2m + 1)\pi - \theta) \), leading to \( (2m+1)\pi - \theta = 6\theta \), which simplifies to \( \theta = \frac{(2m + 1)\pi}{7} \). There are 7 solutions for this case.
Step 6: The second case is \( \sin \theta = \sin(2n\pi + \theta) \), leading to \( 2n\pi + \theta = 6\theta \), which simplifies to \( \theta = \frac{2n\pi}{5} \). There are 5 solutions for this case.
Step 7: Summing the solutions from both cases gives \( 7 + 5 = 12 \) solutions for \( a \).
Step 8: For each solution for \( a \), there are \( 120 \) corresponding solutions for \( z \) (since \( z^{120} = a \)).
Step 9: Thus, the total number of solutions for \( z \) is \( 12 \cdot 120 = 1440 \).
Final answer: \( \boxed{440} \) (remainder when \( 1440 \) is divided by \( 1000 \)).
