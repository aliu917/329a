CORRECT SOLUTION STEPS:
Step 0: Let \( RC = x \). Given values are \( PQ = 735 \) and \( QR = 112 \).
Step 1: From triangle similarity \( \triangle PAQ \sim \triangle PDC \), we write the ratio:
\[

\]
Step 2: From triangle similarity \( \triangle BRQ \sim \triangle DRC \), we write the ratio:
\[
\frac{QR}{RC} = \frac{112}{x} = \frac{CD - AQ}{CD} = 1 - \frac{AQ}{CD}
\]
Step 3: Rearranging the equation gives:
\[
\frac{AQ}{CD} = 1 - \frac{112}{x} = \frac{x - 112}{x}
\]
Step 4: Substitute the expression for \(\frac{AQ}{CD}\) from Step 1 and
Step 3:
\[
\frac{735}{847 + x} = \frac{x - 112}{x}
\]
Step 5: Cross-multiplying gives:
\[
735x = (847 + x)(x - 112)
\]
Step 6: Expanding the right-hand side:
\[
735x = 847x - 112 \cdot 847 + x^2 - 112x
\]
Step 7: Rearranging yields:
\[
0 = x^2 - (847 - 735 + 112)x - 112 \cdot 847
\]
\[
0 = x^2 - 224x - 112 \cdot 847
\]
Step 8: Solving the quadratic equation using the quadratic formula \( x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} \):
\[
0 = x^2 - 224x + 112 \cdot 847
\]
Step 9: Calculate \( 112 \cdot 847 \) and solve for \( x \).
Step 10: Simplifying gives \( RC = \sqrt{112 \cdot 847} = 308 \).

Final answer: \( \boxed{308} \)
