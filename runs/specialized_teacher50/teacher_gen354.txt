Step 1: Draw line segment DE such that DE is parallel to BC, forming an isosceles trapezoid ABED with AD = BE = 10 and BC = 8.
Step 2: Since DE is parallel to BC, the length of EC is calculated as EC = BC - BE = 8 - 6 = 2.
Step 3: Given that DC = 12 and angle CED = 120 degrees, apply the Law of Cosines to triangle CED: 
\[ 
12^2 = DE^2 + 2^2 - 2 \cdot 2 \cdot DE \cdot \cos(120^\circ). 
\]
Step 4: Simplify the equation:
\[ 
144 = DE^2 + 4 + 2DE. 
\]
Step 5: Rearranging gives:
\[ 
DE^2 + 2DE - 140 = 0. 
\]
Step 6: Completing the square or using the quadratic formula, we get:
\[ 
DE = \sqrt{141} - 1. 
\]
Step 7: Determine lengths AP and BQ in triangles DAP and EBQ, which are both 30-60-90 triangles. Hence, AP = 5 and BQ = 5.
Step 8: Calculate the total length of AB:
\[ 
AB = AP + PQ + BQ = 5 + (\sqrt{141} - 1) + 5 = 9 + \sqrt{141}. 
\]
Step 9: Identify p and q in the expression AB = p + \sqrt{q} as p = 9 and q = 141.

Final answer: \( p + q = 9 + 141 = \boxed{150} \).
