Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: We divide the path into eight “\$R\$” movements and eight “\$U\$” movements. Five sections of alternative \$RURUR\$ or \$URURU\$ are necessary in order to make four “turns.” We use the first case and multiply by \$2\$.
For \$U\$, we have seven ordered pairs of positive integers \$(a,b)\$ such that \$a+b=8\$.
For \$R\$, we subtract \$1\$ from each section (to make the minimum stars of each section \$0\$) and we use Stars and Bars to get \${7 \choose 5}=21\$.
Thus our answer is \$7\cdot21\cdot2=\boxed{294}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Define the movements in the grid. Let \$R\$ represent a movement to the right and \$U\$ represent a movement upward. There are 8 \$R\$ movements and 8 \$U\$ movements in total.

Step 1: Recognize that to create paths with exactly four turns, the path must consist of five sections of movements, alternating between \$R\$ and \$U\$ (i.e., \$RURUR\$ or \$URURU\$).

Step 2: Consider one case where the path starts with \$R\$ (i.e., \$RURUR\$) and note that this can be multiplied by 2 for the reverse case starting with \$U\$.

Step 3: For the \$U\$ movements, we need to form seven ordered pairs of positive integers \$(a, b)\$ such that \$a + b = 8\$. Each pair represents the number of \$U\$ movements in each section.

Step 4: For the \$R\$ movements, we need to adjust the sections by subtracting 1 from each section (to ensure each section has at least 0 movements), so we need to find the number of non-negative integer solutions to the equation \$a' + b' + c' + d' + e' = 3\$, where \$a', b', c', d', e'\$ represent the movements in each section after adjustment.

Step 5: Use the Stars and Bars combinatorial method to find the number of ways to distribute 3 indistinguishable stars (remaining \$R\$ movements) into 5 distinguishable bins (the sections), which gives us \${7 \choose 4\ (or\ 3)} = 21\$.

Step 6: Combine the results from the \$U\$ movements and \$R\$ movements. The total number of paths is \$7\$ (for the \$U\$ movements) times \$21\$ (for the \$R\$ movements), multiplied by \$2\$ for the two cases of starting with \$R\$ or \$U\$.

Step 7: Calculate the final result: \$7 \cdot 21 \cdot 2 = 294\$.

Final answer: \(\boxed{294}\)
