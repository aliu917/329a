QUESTION:
Triangle \$ABC\$ has side lengths \$AB=4\$, \$BC=5\$, and \$CA=6\$. Points \$D\$ and \$E\$ are on ray \$AB\$ with \$AB<AD<AE\$. The point \$F \neq C\$ is a point of intersection of the circumcircles of \$\triangle ACD\$ and \$\triangle EBC\$ satisfying \$DF=2\$ and \$EF=7\$. Then \$BE\$ can be expressed as \$\tfrac{a+b\sqrt{c}}{d}\$, where \$a\$, \$b\$, \$c\$, and \$d\$ are positive integers such that \$a\$ and \$d\$ are relatively prime, and \$c\$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find \$a+b+c+d\$.

CORRECT SOLUTION STEPS:
Step 0: Define the variables for the triangle: let \( AB = 4 \), \( BC = 5 \), \( CA = 6 \). Define points \( D \) and \( E \) on ray \( AB \), such that \( AB < AD < AE \). Step 1: Use the Law of Cosines to find \( \cos C \) where \( C \) is the angle at vertex \( C \) of triangle \( ABC \):
\[
\cos C = \frac{AC^2 + BC^2 - AB^2}{2 \cdot AC \cdot BC} = \frac{6^2 + 5^2 - 4^2}{2 \cdot 6 \cdot 5} = \frac{36 + 25 - 16}{60} = \frac{45}{60} = \frac{3}{4}.
\]
Step 2: Calculate \( DE \) using the Law of Cosines as well:
\[
DE^2 = DF^2 + EF^2 - 2 \cdot DF \cdot EF \cdot \cos C.
\]
Substituting the known values:
\[
DE^2 = 2^2 + 7^2 - 2 \cdot 2 \cdot 7 \cdot \frac{3}{4} = 4 + 49 - 21 = 32 \implies DE = 4\sqrt{2}.
\]
Step 3: Let \( X = \overline{AB} \cap \overline{CF} \), and define \( a = XB \) and \( b = XD \). From the intersection properties:
\[
XA \cdot XD = XC \cdot XF = XB \cdot XE.
\]
This leads to the equation:
\[
b(a + 4) = a(b + 4\sqrt{2}).
\]
Step 4: Rearranging gives:
\[
ba + 4b = ab + 4a\sqrt{2} \implies 4b = 4a\sqrt{2} \implies b = a\sqrt{2}.
\]
Step 5: From triangle similarity \( \triangle XFD \sim \triangle XAC \):
\[
XF = \frac{4 + a}{3}.
\]
Step 6: From triangle similarity \( \triangle XFE \sim \triangle XBC \):
\[
\frac{7}{5} = \frac{4 + a}{3a} \implies 7 \cdot 3a = 5(4 + a) \implies 21a = 20 + 5a \implies 16a = 20 \implies a = \frac{5}{4}.
\]
Step 7: Calculate \( BE \):
\[
BE = a + b + 4\sqrt{2} = a + a\sqrt{2} + 4\sqrt{2} = \frac{5}{4} + \frac{5\sqrt{2}}{4} + 4\sqrt{2}.
\]
This simplifies to:
\[
BE = \frac{5 + 5\sqrt{2} + 16\sqrt{2}}{4} = \frac{5 + 21\sqrt{2}}{4}.
\]
Step 8: Identify \( a \), \( b \), \( c \), and \( d \) from \( BE = \frac{a + b\sqrt{c}}{d} \):
Here, \( a = 5 \), \( b = 21 \), \( c = 2 \), and \( d = 4 \).
Step 9: Find the sum \( a + b + c + d \):
\[
5 + 21 + 2 + 4 = 32.
\]

Final answer: \( \boxed{32} \).
