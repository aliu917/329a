CORRECT SOLUTION STEPS:
Step 0: Define the sum \( U = \sum_{n=1}^{2023}\left\lfloor \frac{n^{2}-na}{5}\right\rfloor \) and note that we are looking for a unique positive integer \( a \) such that \( U \) is strictly between -1000 and 1000.
Step 1: Define \( U' = \sum_{n=1}^{2023} \frac{n^2 - na}{5} \).
Step 2: Use the property of the floor function, which states that for any real number \( x \), \( \lfloor x \rfloor \leq x < \lfloor x \rfloor + 1 \). Therefore, we have \( U \leq U' < U + 2023 \).
Step 3: Establish the bounds for \( U' \) based on the condition for \( U \): \( -1000 \leq U < 1000 \) implies \( -1000 \leq U' < 1000 + 2023 = 3023 \).
Step 4: Express \( U' \) in terms of \( a \):
\[
U' = \sum_{n=1}^{2023} \left( \frac{n^2}{5} - \frac{na}{5} \right) = \frac{1}{5} \left( \sum_{n=1}^{2023} n^2 - a \sum_{n=1}^{2023} n \right).
\]
Step 5: Calculate the sums using formulas:
\[
\sum_{n=1}^{2023} n^2 = \frac{2023(2023+1)(4047)}{6}, \quad \sum_{n=1}^{2023} n = \frac{2023(2023+1)}{2}.
\]
Step 6: Substitute these sums into \( U' \):
\[
U' = \frac{1}{5} \left( \frac{2023(2024)(4047)}{6} - a \cdot \frac{2023(2024)}{2} \right).
\]
Step 7: Simplify \( U' \):
\[
U' = \frac{2023(2024)(4047 - 3a)}{30}.
\]
Step 8: Set the bounds for \( 4047 - 3a \) based on the inequalities for \( U' \):
\[
-30000 \leq 2023(2024)(4047 - 3a) \leq 90690.
\]
Step 9: Since \( 2023 \cdot 2024 \) is much larger than \( 90690 \), it follows that \( 4047 - 3a = 0 \) or \( a = 1349 \) is the only viable integer solution.
Step 10: Calculate \( U' \) when \( a = 1349 \):
\[
U' = 0.
\]
Step 11: Now find \( U - U' \):
\[
U - U' = \sum_{n=1}^{2023} \left( \lfloor \frac{n^2 - 1349n}{5} \rfloor - \frac{n^2 - 1349n}{5} \right).
\]
Step 12: Analyze the terms \( n^2 - 1349n \) modulo 5 to find how many of each remainder occur.
Step 13: Calculate the contributions based on these remainders:
- There are 405 numbers each for \( n \equiv 1, 2, 3 \mod 5 \) and 404 for \( n \equiv 0, 4 \mod 5 \).
Step 14: Substitute the values into the expression for \( U - U' \):
\[
U - U' = 0 \times 404 - 405 \cdot \frac{2}{5} - 405 \cdot \frac{1}{5} - 405 \cdot \frac{2}{5} - 0 \cdot 404 = -405.
\]
Step 15: Thus, we have \( U = -405 \).
Step 16: Finally, calculate \( a + U = 1349 - 405 = 944 \).

Final answer: \(\boxed{944}\)
