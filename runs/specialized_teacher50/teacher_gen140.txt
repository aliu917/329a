CORRECT SOLUTION STEPS:

\[

\[

\[

\[

Step 11: Since \( AD \) must be less than \( 10 \), take the solution \( x = \frac{250}{39} \).
Step 12: Express \( AD \) in the form \( \frac{p}{q} \) where \( p = 250 \) and \( q = 39 \).
Step 13: Finally, calculate \( p + q = 250 + 39 = 289 \).
