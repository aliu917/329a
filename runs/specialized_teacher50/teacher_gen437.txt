Question: Two distinct, real, infinite geometric series each have a sum of \$1\$ and have the same second term. The third term of one of the series is \$1/8\$, and the second term of both series can be written in the form \$\frac{\sqrt{m}-n}p\$, where \$m\$, \$n\$, and \$p\$ are positive integers and \$m\$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find \$100m+10n+p\$.

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: Let the second term of each series be \$x\$. Then, the common ratio is \$\frac{1}{8x}\$, and the first term is \$8x^2\$.
So, the sum is \$\frac{8x^2}{1-\frac{1}{8x}}=1\$. Thus, \$64x^3-8x+1 = (4x-1)(16x^2+4x-1) = 0 \Rightarrow x = \frac{1}{4}, \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{8}\$.
The only solution in the appropriate form is \$x = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{8}\$. Therefore, \$100m+10n+p = \boxed{518}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Let the second term of each series be \( x \). The first term and common ratio will be expressed in terms of \( x \).

Step 1: The common ratio of the series is \( \frac{1}{8x} \) since the third term of one series is given as \( \frac{1}{8} \).

Step 2: The first term of the series can be calculated as \( 8x^2 \) based on the relationship between the terms in a geometric series.

Step 3: The sum of the infinite geometric series can be expressed using the formula \( S = \frac{a}{1 - r} \), where \( a \) is the first term and \( r \) is the common ratio. Thus, we set up the equation: 
\[ 
\frac{8x^2}{1 - \frac{1}{8x}} = 1 
\]

Step 4: Simplify the equation from Step 3 to find \( x \):
\[ 
8x^2 = 1 - \frac{1}{8x} 
\]
Multiplying through by \( 8x \) to eliminate the fraction gives:
\[ 
64x^3 - 8x + 1 = 0 
\]

Step 5: Factor the cubic equation from Step 4. We can factor it as:
\[ 
(4x - 1)(16x^2 + 4x - 1) = 0 
\]

Step 6: Solve for \( x \) from the factors. This gives:
\[ 
4x - 1 = 0 \Rightarrow x = \frac{1}{4} 
\]
And for the quadratic:
\[ 
16x^2 + 4x - 1 = 0 
\]
The solutions can be found using the quadratic formula:
\[ 
x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} = \frac{-4 \pm \sqrt{16 + 64}}{32} = \frac{-4 \pm \sqrt{80}}{32} = \frac{-4 \pm 4\sqrt{5}}{32} = \frac{-1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{8}
\]

Step 7: Select the appropriate solution. The only valid solution for \( x \) that is positive in the required form is:
\[ 
x = \frac{\sqrt{5} - 1}{8} 
\]

Step 8: Identify \( m \), \( n \), and \( p \) from the expression \( x = \frac{\sqrt{m}-n}{p} \):
- Here, \( m = 5 \), \( n = 1 \), \( p = 8 \).

Step 9: Compute \( 100m + 10n + p \):
\[ 
100 \cdot 5 + 10 \cdot 1 + 8 = 500 + 10 + 8 = 518 
\]

Final answer: \(\boxed{518}\)
