QUESTION:
Square \$ ABCD \$ has sides of length 1. Points \$ E \$ and \$ F \$ are on \$ \overline{BC} \$ and \$ \overline{CD}, \$ respectively, so that \$ \triangle AEF \$ is equilateral. A square with vertex \$ B \$ has sides that are parallel to those of \$ ABCD \$ and a vertex on \$ \overline{AE}. \$ The length of a side of this smaller square is \$\frac{a-\sqrt{b}}{c}, \$ where \$ a, b, \$ and \$ c \$ are positive integers and \$ b\$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find \$ a+b+c. \$

CORRECT SOLUTION STEPS:
Step 0: Define the vertices of square \( ABCD \) as \( A = (0, 1) \), \( B = (0, 0) \), \( C = (1, 0) \), and \( D = (1, 1) \). Denote the coordinates of points \( E \) and \( F \) on segments \( \overline{BC} \) and \( \overline{CD} \) respectively.
Step 1: Let \( E = (1, y_E) \) and \( F = (x_F, 0) \) where \( 0 \leq y_E \leq 1 \) and \( 0 \leq x_F \leq 1 \).
Step 2: Since triangle \( AEF \) is equilateral, the lengths \( AE \), \( EF \), and \( AF \) must be equal.
Step 3: Calculate the lengths \( AE \), \( EF \), and \( AF \):
- For \( AE \): \( AE = \sqrt{(0 - 1)^2 + (1 - y_E)^2} = \sqrt{1 + (1 - y_E)^2} \).
- For \( AF \): \( AF = \sqrt{(0 - x_F)^2 + (1 - 0)^2} = \sqrt{x_F^2 + 1} \).
- For \( EF \): \( EF = \sqrt{(1 - x_F)^2 + (y_E - 0)^2} = \sqrt{(1 - x_F)^2 + y_E^2} \).
Step 4: Set the lengths equal to each other: 
- \( AE = AF \): \( \sqrt{1 + (1 - y_E)^2} = \sqrt{x_F^2 + 1} \) leads to the equation \( 1 + (1 - y_E)^2 = x_F^2 + 1 \).
Step 5: Simplifying gives:
- \( (1 - y_E)^2 = x_F^2 \) or \( 1 - y_E = x_F \) (assuming non-negative values).
Step 6: Next, set \( AE = EF \): \( \sqrt{1 + (1 - y_E)^2} = \sqrt{(1 - x_F)^2 + y_E^2} \).
Step 7: Squaring both sides gives: 
- \( 1 + (1 - y_E)^2 = (1 - x_F)^2 + y_E^2 \).
Step 8: Substitute \( y_E = 1 - x_F \) into the equation:
- This results in a single variable equation in terms of \( x_F \).
Step 9: Solve for \( x_F \) and find \( y_E \), leading to values for \( AE \) and \( EF \).
Step 10: The side length \( s \) of the smaller square is derived from the previous equalities and calculations, giving \( s = \frac{3 - \sqrt{3}}{6} \).
Step 11: Identify \( a, b, c \) from the expression \( s = \frac{a - \sqrt{b}}{c} \) which leads to \( a = 3, b = 3, c = 6 \).

Final answer: \( a + b + c = 3 + 3 + 6 = \boxed{12} \).
