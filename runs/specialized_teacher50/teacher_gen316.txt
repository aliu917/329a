Step 1: By SAS Congruence, triangle \( ABD \) is congruent to triangle \( ACD \), so \( \angle ADB = \angle ADC = 90^\circ \).
Step 2: The angle \( \angle IBD \) is half of \( \angle ABD \), denoted as \( \theta \).
Step 3: Using the cosine of \( \theta \), we have \( \cos(\theta) = \frac{y}{8} \).
Step 4: Using the double angle formula for cosine, \( \cos(2\theta) = \frac{y}{x} = 2\cos^2(\theta) - 1 = \frac{y^2 - 32}{32} \).
Step 5: Cross-multiplying gives us the equation \( 32y = x(y^2 - 32) \).
Step 6: Since \( x, y > 0 \), it follows that \( y^2 - 32 \) must be positive, leading to \( y > 5.5 \).
Step 7: Given that triangle \( IBD \) has hypotenuse \( \overline{IB} \) of length \( 8 \), we also know \( BD = y < 8 \).
Step 8: Since \( BC = 2y \) must be an integer, the possible integer values for \( y \) are \( 6 \), \( 7 \), as \( 6.5 \) and \( 7.5 \) are not integers.
Step 9: Testing \( y = 6 \), we substitute into the equation \( 32y = x(y^2 - 32) \) to find \( x \):
\[
x = \frac{32(6)}{(6)^2 - 32} = \frac{192}{36 - 32} = \frac{192}{4} = 48.
\]
Step 10: Calculate the perimeter of triangle \( ABC \):
\[
\text{Perimeter} = AB + AC + BC = x + x + 2y = 2x + 2y = 2(48) + 2(6) = 96 + 12 = 108.
