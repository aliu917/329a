Question: Let \$\omega\neq 1\$ be a 13th root of unity. Find the remainder when
\prod_{k=0}^{12}(2-2\omega^k+\omega^{2k})
is divided by 1000.

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: \prod_{k=0}^{12} \left(2- 2\omega^k + \omega^{2k}\right) = \prod_{k=0}^{12} \left((1 - \omega^k)^2 + 1\right) = \prod_{k=0}^{12} \left((1 + i) - \omega^k)((1 - i) - \omega^k\right)
Now, we consider the polynomial \$x^{13} - 1\$ whose roots are the 13th roots of unity. Taking our rewritten product from \$0\$ to \$12\$, we see that both instances of \$\omega^k\$ cycle through each of the 13th roots. Then, our answer is:
((1 + i)^{13} - 1)(1 - i)^{13} - 1)
= (-64(1 + i) - 1)(-64(1 - i) - 1)
= (65 + 64i)(65 - 64i)
= 65^2 + 64^2
= 8\boxed{\textbf{321}}

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Let \(\omega \neq 1\) be a 13th root of unity. 

Step 1: Rewrite the product \(\prod_{k=0}^{12}(2 - 2\omega^k + \omega^{2k})\) as \(\prod_{k=0}^{12}((1 - \omega^k)^2 + 1)\).

Step 2: Recognize that \((1 - \omega^k)^2 + 1\) can be factored as \(\prod_{k=0}^{12}((1 + i) - \omega^k)((1 - i) - \omega^k)\).

Step 3: Consider the polynomial \(x^{13} - 1\) whose roots are the 13th roots of unity, which includes \(\omega^k\) for \(k = 0, 1, ..., 12\).

Step 4: Note that both instances of \(\omega^k\) cycle through each of the 13th roots in the product.

Step 5: Compute \((1 + i)^{13} - 1\) and \((1 - i)^{13} - 1\).

Step 6: Calculate \((1 + i)^{13}\) using the polar form: \(1 + i = \sqrt{2} e^{i\frac{\pi}{4}}\), thus \((1 + i)^{13} = (\sqrt{2})^{13} e^{i\frac{13\pi}{4}} = 2^{6.5} e^{i\frac{13\pi}{4}}\).

Step 7: Simplify \(e^{i\frac{13\pi}{4}} = e^{i\frac{5\pi}{4}} = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} - i\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\).

Step 8: Therefore, \((1 + i)^{13} = 64\sqrt{2}(-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} - i\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}})\).

Step 9: Similarly calculate \((1 - i)^{13}\).

Step 10: Combine results: \((-64(1 + i) - 1)(-64(1 - i) - 1)\).

Step 11: The result can be expressed as \((65 + 64i)(65 - 64i)\).

Step 12: Calculate the product: \(65^2 + 64^2\).

Step 13: Compute \(65^2 = 4225\) and \(64^2 = 4096\).

Step 14: Add the squared values: \(4225 + 4096 = 8321\).

Step 15: Find the remainder when \(8321\) is divided by \(1000\) which is \(321\).

Final answer: \(\boxed{321}\)
