Since \$A = (u,v)\$, we can find the coordinates of the other points: \$B = (v,u)\$, \$C = (-v,u)\$, \$D = (-v,-u)\$, \$E = (v,-u)\$. If we graph those points, we notice that since the latter four points are all reflected across the x/y-axis, they form a rectangle, and \$ABE\$ is a triangle. The area of \$BCDE\$ is \$(2u)(2v) = 4uv\$ and the area of \$ABE\$ is \$\frac{1}{2}(2u)(u-v) = u^2 - uv\$. Adding these together, we get \$u^2 + 3uv = u(u+3v) = 451 = 11 \cdot 41\$. Since \$u,v\$ are positive, \$u+3v>u\$, and by matching factors we get either \$(u,v) = (1,150)\$ or \$(11,10)\$. Since \$v < u\$ the latter case is the answer, and \$u+v = \boxed{021}\$.
=

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Denote the coordinates of point A as \( A = (u, v) \). 

Step 1: Determine the coordinates of point B, which is the reflection of A across the line \( y = x \): \( B = (v, u) \).

Step 2: Determine the coordinates of point C, which is the reflection of B across the y-axis: \( C = (-v, u) \).

Step 3: Determine the coordinates of point D, which is the reflection of C across the x-axis: \( D = (-v, -u) \).

Step 4: Determine the coordinates of point E, which is the reflection of D across the y-axis: \( E = (v, -u) \).

Step 5: Recognize that points B, C, D, and E form a rectangle and point A forms a triangle with points B and E.

Step 6: Calculate the area of rectangle BCDE, which is given by \( (2u)(2v) = 4uv \).

Step 7: Calculate the area of triangle ABE using the formula for the area of a triangle: \( \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times 2u \times (u - v) = u^2 - uv \).

Step 8: Add the areas of the rectangle and triangle together: \( \text{Total Area} = 4uv + (u^2 - uv) = u^2 + 3uv \).

Step 9: Set the total area equal to 451: \( u^2 + 3uv = 451 \).

Step 10: Factor the equation: \( u(u + 3v) = 451 \).

Step 11: Identify the factor pairs of 451. The factors are \( 1 \times 451 \) and \( 11 \times 41 \).

Step 12: Analyze the factor pairs:
- For \( (u, u + 3v) = (1, 451) \): This gives \( v = \frac{450}{3} = 150 \), which does not satisfy \( v < u \).
- For \( (u, u + 3v) = (11, 41) \): This gives \( 3v = 41 - 11 = 30 \), thus \( v = 10 \).

Step 13: Since \( v < u \) holds true with \( u = 11 \) and \( v = 10 \), we conclude these values are valid.

Step 14: Calculate \( u + v = 11 + 10 = 21 \).

Final answer: \(\boxed{21}\)
