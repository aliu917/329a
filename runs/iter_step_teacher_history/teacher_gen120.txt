Question: Triangle \$ABC\$ has \$AB=27\$, \$AC=26\$, and \$BC=25\$.  Let \$I\$ be the intersection of the internal angle bisectors of \$\triangle ABC\$.  What is \$BI\$?
\$ \textbf{(A)}\ 15\qquad\textbf{(B)}\ 5+\sqrt{26}+3\sqrt{3}\qquad\textbf{(C)}\ 3\sqrt{26}\qquad\textbf{(D)}\ \frac{2}{3}\sqrt{546}\qquad\textbf{(E)}\ 9\sqrt{3} \$

Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: Inscribe circle \$C\$ of radius \$r\$ inside triangle \$ABC\$ so that it meets \$AB\$ at \$Q\$, \$BC\$ at \$R\$, and \$AC\$ at \$S\$. Note that angle bisectors of triangle \$ABC\$ are concurrent at the center \$O\$(also \$I\$) of circle \$C\$. Let \$x=QB\$, \$y=RC\$ and \$z=AS\$. Note that \$BR=x\$, \$SC=y\$ and \$AQ=z\$. Hence \$x+z=27\$, \$x+y=25\$, and \$z+y=26\$. Subtracting the last 2 equations we have \$x-z=-1\$ and adding this to the first equation we have \$x=13\$.
By Heron's formula for the area of a triangle we have that the area of triangle \$ABC\$ is \$\sqrt{39(14)(13)(12)}\$. On the other hand the area is given by \$(1/2)25r+(1/2)26r+(1/2)27r\$.  Then \$39r=\sqrt{39(14)(13)(12)}\$ so that \$r^2=56\$.
Since the radius of circle \$O\$ is perpendicular to \$BC\$ at \$R\$, we have by the pythagorean theorem \$BO^2=BI^2=r^2+x^2=56+169=225\$ so that \$BI=\boxed{\textbf{(A) } 15}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Inscribe circle \$C\$ of radius \$r\$ inside triangle \$ABC\$ so that it meets \$AB\$ at \$Q\$, \$BC\$ at \$R\$, and \$AC\$ at \$S\$.

Step 1: The angle bisectors of triangle \$ABC\$ are concurrent at the center \$O\$ (also \$I\$) of circle \$C\$.

Step 2: Let \$x=QB\$, \$y=RC\$, and \$z=AS\$.

Step 3: Establish relationships based on the lengths of the sides: \$x + z = 27\$, \$x + y = 25\$, and \$z + y = 26\$.

Step 4: Subtract the last two equations: \$x - z = -1\$.

Step 5: Add this result to the first equation: \$x = 13\$.

Step 6: Use Heron's formula to find the area of triangle \$ABC\$: \$\text{Area} = \sqrt{39(14)(13)(12)}\$.

Step 7: The area can also be expressed as \$(1/2) \cdot 25r + (1/2) \cdot 26r + (1/2) \cdot 27r = 39r\$.

Step 8: Set the two area expressions equal: \$39r = \sqrt{39(14)(13)(12)}\$.

Step 9: Solve for \$r^2\$ to find that \$r^2 = 56\$.

Step 10: Since the radius of circle \$O\$ is perpendicular to \$BC\$ at \$R\$, apply the Pythagorean theorem: \$BO^2 = BI^2 = r^2 + x^2 = 56 + 169 = 225\$.

Step 11: Find \$BI\$ by taking the square root of \$225$: \$BI = 15\$.

Final answer: \(\boxed{15}\)
