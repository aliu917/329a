Solution chain-of-thought reasoning: The area of the equilateral triangle is \$\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\$. The area of the three squares is \$3\times 1=3\$.
Since \$\angle C=360\$, \$\angle GCH=360-90-90-60=120\$.
Dropping an altitude from \$C\$ to \$GH\$ allows to create a \$30-60-90\$ triangle since \$\triangle GCH\$ is isosceles. This means that the height of \$\triangle GCH\$ is \$\dfrac{1}{2}\$ and half the length of \$GH\$ is \$\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\$. Therefore, the area of each isosceles triangle is \$\dfrac{1}{2}\times\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\$. Multiplying by \$3\$ yields \$\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}\$ for all three isosceles triangles.
Therefore, the total area is \$3+\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}+\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{4}=3+\dfrac{4\sqrt{3}}{4}=3+\sqrt{3}\implies\boxed{\textbf{(C)}\ 3+\sqrt3}\$.

Solution steps:
--------------------------------------------------
Step 0: Calculate the area of the equilateral triangle \( \triangle ABC \) with side length 1. The area is given by \( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \).

Step 1: Calculate the area of the three squares \( ABDE \), \( BCHI \), and \( CAFG \) which are each \( 1 \times 1 \). The total area of the three squares is \( 3 \times 1 = 3 \).

Step 2: Determine the angles around point \( C \). Since \( \angle C = 360^\circ \), we calculate \( \angle GCH \) as \( 360 - 90 - 90 - 60 = 120^\circ \).

Step 3: Drop an altitude from point \( C \) to line \( GH \). This creates a \( 30^\circ-60^\circ-90^\circ \) triangle, \( \triangle GCH \).

Step 4: In triangle \( GCH \), the height from \( C \) to \( GH \) is \( \frac{1}{2} \), and half the length of \( GH \) (opposite the \( 30^\circ \) angle) is \( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \).

Step 5: Calculate the area of triangle \( GCH \) using the formula for the area of a triangle: \( \frac{1}{2} \times \text{base} \times \text{height} = \frac{1}{2} \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \).

Step 6: Thus, the area of triangle \( GCH \) is \( \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \).

Step 7: Since there are three such isosceles triangles around point \( C \), multiply the area of one triangle by 3 to find the total area contributed by these triangles: \( 3 \times \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4} \).

Step 8: Calculate the total area of the hexagon \( DEFGHI \) by summing the area of the equilateral triangle and the areas of the three squares and isosceles triangles: 
\[ 
3 + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} + \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{4} = 3 + \frac{4\sqrt{3}}{4} = 3 + \sqrt{3} 
\]

Final answer: \(\boxed{3 + \sqrt{3}}\)
